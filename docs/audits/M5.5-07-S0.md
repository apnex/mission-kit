`compare` on `s0-02`: no regressions.

**The suite discriminated weakly.**\
Six of seven probes scored full marks on the old charter, because readers could reach the answers from the members, `M0` and the old faults list.\
Only Q5 - whether a source-code comment rule exists and what it would trigger - moved, from 1 to 2.\
So the measured gain is one probe; the Territory, Composition and Neighbours sections are justified by `E4` conformance, not by this score.

**The correction.**\
In `s0-01` readers found the source-code gap but did not connect it to the single-medium fault.\
One sentence in Gaps now says either gap would be a new medium and the second single-medium rule; Q5 then reached 2 in all three after-readers.

**Other movement is noise.**\
`layer-charters.H7` and `system-boundary.S4` rose by one reader each; neither probe reads text this change touched.
