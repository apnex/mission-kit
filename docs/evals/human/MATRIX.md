## Evidence levels

- **untested** - proposed, no human evidence.
- **package** - preferred by the human as part of a message changing several moves at once; the move's own share is unknown.
- **isolated** - preferred in a pair that differs in that move alone.
- **settled** - isolated and preferred across several sessions and moments; guidance.
- **retired** - tested and not preferred; dropped from guidance.
