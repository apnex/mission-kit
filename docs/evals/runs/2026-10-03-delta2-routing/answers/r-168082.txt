### component-a3.C5
A3 names this exact shape as a fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns." For a change that falls short of the ideal, the Mandate says "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches", where "them" means "god objects, spaghetti coupling and dual-purpose modules". So your feature must not go into `utils`, and if your change touches part of `utils`, A3 asks you to remove that god-object part, for example by moving the concern you touch into its own single-concern unit. Under "Law of One" the feature gets its own module that "does exactly one thing", and under "Composable by Default" it should be "assembled by composing existing units, never by modifying them". Under "Earned Exposure" the new unit "earns an internal boundary by being one concern", but it must not be promoted to "a stable, depended-upon surface" until "a real consumer outside its origin needs it" (otherwise it is a "Speculative Surface"). NOT SETTLED: whether you must split up the parts of `utils` your change does not touch. The text binds only "what it touches", and says "How an axiom binds a change is stated in A0", which I was not allowed to read. GUESS: the untouched remainder of `utils` is out of scope for your change.

### OBSERVATIONS
- "removes what it touches" is ambiguous. It could mean the god-object/coupling defects your change touches, or every part of the module your change touches, and the file does not say how much clean-up counts as "removing".
- There is a tension between "removes what it touches" (cleaning up `utils` means modifying it) and "Composable by Default": "never by modifying them". The file does not reconcile the two.
- The binding rule for a change is deferred to A0 (`README.md`), and "Bypass as Signal" also points to `A0`. Neither was available, so how strongly A3 binds this change cannot be settled from this file alone.
