### component-a3.C5
The 'utils' module is a named fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns."
A3 asks that your change not add to it: "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches" - so the new feature must not be dropped into 'utils' as another unrelated concern, and per the "Law of One" it belongs in a unit that "does exactly one thing".
It should arrive by composition: "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by modifying them", interacting only "through declared adapters and contracts" (Air-Gap Principle).
The new concern "earns an internal boundary by being one concern", but should not be exposed as a stable surface unless "a real consumer outside its origin needs it" (Earned Exposure).
If your change touches 'utils', "removes what it touches" asks you to remove the accreted concern(s) you touch; NOT SETTLED is how far that goes (only the parts you touch, versus splitting the whole module), and the file defers "How an axiom binds a change" to A0, which I did not read.
GUESS: A3 does not require you to refactor all of 'utils' for a small unrelated feature - only to avoid adding to it and to clean up the parts your change actually touches.
