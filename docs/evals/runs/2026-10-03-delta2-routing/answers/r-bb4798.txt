### work-layers.L3
It goes in `work-types/` (W), and `work-types/W10-adversarial-design-review-upstream.md` already covers it.
`W0` says a work-type carries `roleEligibility`, `domainEligibility`, `evidenceContract` and `falsifier`. That is exactly who may do it, what it may target, and what proof closes it. A candidate is "taxonomy-grade **iff it compiles to a closeable claimable node**".

For who: `roleEligibility: [verifier]`, and `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation` needs an attester who "cannot be the executor given the live roster".
For what: `domainEligibility` lists all seven domains (`domainFreedom: free`), and `target` is "the design under review".

`W9` (audit-a-surface) is the nearby entry for sweeping an existing surface, not a pre-build proposal.

### work-layers.L4
It is a methodology entry, and `methods/M1-triangulated-review.md` already covers it: "require **at least four genuinely independent inputs** before reaching a verdict".
M0's placement question 2 says that guidance whose steps "produce a result of its own - a verdict" is a procedure. M0 places M1 under "Verifying" and calls it "the independence the verifying procedures specialise".

It is not a work-type, because a work-type is a claimable unit with an evidence contract (W0). Independence is already built into the work-type layer as W0 constraint 2 ("Independence is structural + roster-aware") and constraint 8 ("Author != approver"). It is applied in W8 and W13.

### work-layers.L5
It goes at M0's sixth moment, "handing over". The layer depends on its shape. A procedure that produces a handover result goes in `methods/`. A requirement that a record carry something goes in `rules/`. Guidance that leaves no trace goes in `practices/`.
The corpus records the gap explicitly. `methods/README.md`: "handing work in progress to another agent or a later session has no procedure at all", and "a lone agent hands over to its own next session". It treats this as a thin moment: "Neither is a defect until something fails there, and both are where to look first when something does".
`rules/README.md`: "A rule for handing over - *a handover record names what is unfinished* - would leave a trace, and none exists".
`practices/README.md`: "*handing over* hold[s] no practice today", and "A practice is not minted to fill a moment".
Two entries are related but not handover guidance. `axioms/A4-zero-loss-knowledge.md` gives the limit ("A cold pickup from the record alone reproduces the same outputs as a warm actor"), and `traits/T1-stateful.md` treats an agent's notes for its next session as persistent state.
`methods/M10` covers only handover of understanding and decisions to a human.

### work-layers.L6
Method versus skill: M0 says a method's steps "produce a result of its own - a verdict, an artifact, a record", and that guidance is "followed". K0 says a skill is "*invoked*, not followed" and "packages a procedure so an agent can install and invoke it, and earns its packaging where scripts or assets make it necessary".
Work-type: W0 says a work-type is a "verb-famil[y] carrying the *mode* + *shape* of work" and is "taxonomy-grade **iff it compiles to a closeable claimable node**", with roles, domains, an evidence contract and a falsifier. The charter (`README.md`) says the W layer holds "Units of work and their evidence contracts".
Method and skill are the hard pair, and the corpus says so. M0's faults list includes "The procedure that is really a skill". K0 says "A skill that only describes is a method ... as `M9` was before it moved", and `skills/K1` is now superseded by M9.
K3 shows the blur: it is a skill entry but is written as "## Rule ... When to apply", the way a method is.
Method and work-type also sit close, for example M7 axiom-alignment audit against W22 axiom-alignment-gate, and M1 against W8/W13. NOT SETTLED: no text gives a combined three-way test. M0's placement order (axiom, procedure, style, rule, practice) does not mention work-types at all.

### work-layers.L7
`RU1` (rules/): default-reject, so land only what "delivers more value than it costs, measured concretely". Then lead the report with yield: "how many candidates considered, how many landed, what concrete bugs were fixed, what was verified vs. asserted", before any narrative (its `trace`).
`RU3` (rules/): each of the three cuts records an observable revival trigger at the moment of cutting. "Terminal is reopenable", and revival re-triages.
`AR5` (artifacts/): the cuts go in a backlog as retired rows with "reason cut | revives on". A row is never deleted, and its trigger may not be "a date or the word 'later'". `RU2` (rules/): leave the sweep's design records frozen, using banners rather than rewrites.
For the two landings: `M1` (methods/) if they ship to production or upstream (four independent inputs), and `W6` (work-types/) for landing an approved change on main, with the review gate upstream of the merge.
For the report: `S15` (style/) says to put the conclusion first, explain unfamiliar IDs, put any decision in a frame with options, costs and a labelled recommendation, and end with picks. `M10` (methods/) applies if the director must decide, and records the decision (`AR4`). Both are members of set `ST1`.
`A13` (axioms/) says to surface "decision over status" and never narrow the director's options. `R4` (roles/) says ratification belongs to the director alone.
GUESS: A13 binds only if the system is multi-agent or autonomous (its `applies-to`). ST1 also flags a tension: "`RU1` honest yield is triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report", while S15 says to put the point first.
