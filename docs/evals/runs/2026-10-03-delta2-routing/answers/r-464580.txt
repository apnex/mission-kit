### OBSERVATIONS
- Picks versus options: S15 asks you to "End with two or three picks", using Continue/Explore/Return, and its decision frame also asks for "a pick" among options that must include doing nothing. With three options plus doing nothing, it is unclear whether the decision options replace the Continue/Explore/Return picks or sit alongside them, and the 2-3 limit cannot then hold.
- Status updates: A13 says status-chatter must not reach the director ("decision over status"). S15 and M10 assume messages that report where things stand. No entry reconciles the two for a requested progress update.
- When A13 binds: A13's frontmatter says `applies-to: [multi-agent, autonomous]`, so whether it binds depends on the traits of the system in question. Its index trigger ("consume the director's attention") reads as universal.
- ST1's own record: ST1 lists bad news and teaching as gaps, and the tensions with K5 (three questions a round), AR3 (many moves at once) and RU1 are unresolved. Q1 and Q4 have no entry specific to them.
- W24 is missing from ST1 related: ST1's prose names W24 as related, but its `related` frontmatter leaves W24 out.
- Evidence for S15 and M10: both cite `docs/evals/human/...` paths, which are not in this corpus, and the evidence comes from one human on four scenarios.
- External links: INDEX.md says to resolve links against GitHub raw if no checkout exists. I did not do that and read only local files.
