### explain-routing.Q5
S15 gives the form: the first line is the decision being asked for; the decision frame is "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". The deadline belongs under "why now".
M10 step 4: "one decision per message"; all real options and doing nothing are included, "none is hidden" (checking this is M10's job, not S15's); step 5: record the decision and reasoning, and say where in one line.
A13: you may recommend but must not narrow the choices or pre-empt the decision; the director "always sees the full, unbiased option set", and any preference lean is "a labeled tie-break input"; the decide act is non-delegable.
ST1 routes to both; W23 and AR4 are related, not members: W23 captures and ratifies the decision (needs a director signal, "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative"), and AR4 is the record (refuted alternatives, status, authority).
R4 matches if you might act for the director: ratification is the director's alone.
NOT SETTLED: what happens if the deadline passes with no answer. GUESS: A13's "never goes silently dark" means you raise it again rather than decide yourself.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 and M10 cite evidence at docs/evals/human/runs/guidance-1-* and docs/evals/human/MATRIX.md; the corpus root has no docs/ directory (checked by listing), so this evidence cannot be read from the corpus.
- S15 asks for the final picks to be Continue/Explore/Return, but a decision between N options needs N option picks; how these combine (Q5) is not stated.
- A13 says status-chatter never reaches the director and wants "decision over status", while M10/S15 support explaining to a director who has not followed the work; a pure progress update (Q2) falls between them, and the boundary is not settled.
- ST1 records tensions it has not resolved: K5's three questions per round against one question at a time, and AR3's many moves against one decision. Its recorded gaps (bad news and teaching tested in one pair each) bear directly on Q1 and Q4.
- A13 is tagged applies-to [multi-agent, autonomous]; the corpus does not say whether it binds a single agent writing to the director outside such a system.
- S5 (no dates in prose) and S6 (one sentence per line in markdown) are triggered by surface features of a message, but their text is about documents; S0 says S15 is the first rule for a medium other than documents, so whether document rules carry over to messages is unclear.
