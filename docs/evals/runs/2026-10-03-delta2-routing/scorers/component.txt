--- answer a-bb61dd44 ---
No. "A component's directory addresses it ... Its boundary is its declared interfaces" (E6, "Boundary against directory"), and "A directory places a component's documents; it is the boundary of neither the system nor the component" (E5). parts/thing/ is still a component, but "nothing separates it yet" (E6 Definition). The undeclared dependency "is itself a fault against A3" (E5; A0), which violates A3's Air-Gap Principle: "no unit reaches into another's internals". The fix is to declare thing's interface and move other's dependency onto it: "Declaring the boundary, and moving the dependency onto it, is what makes them two again" (E5). A3's "Bypass as Signal" also asks you to check that the interface covers the component's duty, so that the reaching-in is not simply moved somewhere else.
