### explain-routing.Q1
The entry point is ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md), which INDEX.md routes to when "You are about to communicate with a human who has limited context". It names its two members: S15 for "the form of any message to a human" and M10 for "leading a human to understanding, then a decision".
S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks for "Point first: the first line is the conclusion", so the slip goes in the first line. It also asks for one step and one question at the end, explained names, and two or three Continue/Explore/Return picks.
M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks that the human see "what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra". If a decision follows from the slip, M10 asks for it in S15's decision frame, including the cost of doing nothing, and for the decision to be recorded.
A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) says the system must "never [go] silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed", and that "under-surfacing is as much a fault as over-surfacing".
S0 routes here too ("a message a human will read") and points on to S15.
NOT SETTLED: there is no entry for bad news as such. ST1 records "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair; no move is isolated".

### explain-routing.Q2

RU1 is related to ST1 but not a member. ST1 says it "is triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report". Its lead-with-yield rule (rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md) therefore applies only if the update summarises a sweep, review or audit.
GUESS: if the update asks for nothing, A13's warning against attention leaks suggests checking whether it needs to reach the director at all.

### explain-routing.Q5
ST1, S15 and M10 apply. S15's decision frame asks for "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick".
The deadline is the "why now". S15 also says "each option is a Continue pick naming it", so three options make three Continue picks. Explore and Return keep their meanings, and "A request for the human to act is never hidden in an optional pick".
M10 step 4 asks that "none is hidden". It also asks for one decision per message, and that the director "can answer with a pick, not a paragraph". Step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning and say where in one line.
A13 asks for "the full, unbiased option set". The agent "never reworks the option set, narrows the choices, nor pre-empts the decision".
AR4 (the decision record) and W23 (capture and ratify) are where the recorded decision goes, per M10's "When to apply".
Ambiguity: S15 says "two or three picks", but three options as Continue picks plus Explore or Return gives four or five; see OBSERVATIONS.

### explain-routing.Q6
ST1, S15 and M10 apply. M10's Decide limit says "one decision at a time", and step 4 says "Ask for one decision per message".
So the first decision goes in its own message, in S15's decision frame. Record it (M10 step 5) before you ask for the second.
M10 step 3 says "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first, and confirm it has landed". M10 step 1 asks you to say how many steps remain, and S15 asks you to "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line". Together these tell the director that a second decision follows.
A13 forbids pre-empting or narrowing the second decision on the director's behalf.
AR4 and W23 hold each recorded ruling; AR4 has an amends/supersedes field if the second ruling changes the first.
Tensions ST1 records: AR3 "offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time", and K5 "asks three questions a round, against one question at a time"; neither is reconciled.
NOT SETTLED: no text says how to state that the second decision depends on the first, or whether to preview it.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says to end with "two or three picks", and also that when deciding, "each option is a Continue pick naming it" with Explore and Return keeping their meaning. With three options, that gives three Continue picks plus at least one other, which breaks the "two or three" cap. The corpus does not reconcile this.
- Routing depends on time pressure: S15's trigger ("a human will read without the context you have") and A13's are reached from INDEX.md rows, but none of the six moments (bad news, update, why, teaching, deadline, chained decisions) is named in any trigger. ST1 itself records that bad news and teaching were each tested in only one pair.
- S0's trigger covers "a message a human will read", but S6, S5 and S11 have triggers written around markdown prose and documents. S5's trigger ("name a version, date or other point-in-time identifier in prose") could misroute a deadline date in a message, although its body scopes it to user-facing docs. It is unclear which document-style rules apply to messages.
- RU1, K5 and AR3 are listed in ST1 as related with unreconciled tensions. A cold agent reaching them from INDEX.md could get the opposite instruction, the fault ST1 itself names as "Opposite instructions".
- M10 and S15 cite evidence under docs/evals/human/..., which is not in this corpus, so the evidence claims could not be checked.
- INDEX.md says to fetch from a remote URL when no checkout is present. Under this task's constraints I used only the local copy.
