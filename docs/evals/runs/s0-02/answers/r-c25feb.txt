### explain-routing.Q1
Main entries: ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) gathers the "Explain" set, made up of S15 and M10, for the moment you "communicate with a human who has limited context". S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks for "Point first: the first line is the conclusion". Here that means the slip itself, then one step and one question at the end, explained names, references kept on one line, and two or three Continue/Explore/Return picks. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks you to lead the director so they see "what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", one confirmed step at a time, with depth offered but "never pushed". A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) asks for "digest over dump, decision over status" and says the system "never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed". So the slip must be surfaced, without status chatter. S0 (style/README.md) applies because its trigger covers "a message a human will read". NOT SETTLED: no entry gives guidance written for bad news. ST1 records "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair; no move is isolated" as a gap.

### explain-routing.Q2
ST1 sends you to S15 and M10, and M10's "When to apply" says "especially when they have not followed the work". S15 asks that the first line be the conclusion. It also asks you to "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line", to explain or drop any name the director has not seen, and to "Leave out what you did when it does not change what the human understands or decides". M10 asks you to know the finish line, "say how many steps remain", give one step per message, and stop at a pause point. A13 warns against "Attention Leak": status chatter and ceremony should not reach the director, and what does reach them should be "decision-shaped and gate-aligned". S0 also applies, as the style layer's entry point for messages to a human. RU1 (rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md) asks you to lead with the delivered outcome, but ST1 says it "is triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report". So it applies only if the update closes out a sweep or audit. GUESS: its yield-first opening fits S15's point-first rule in any case.

### explain-routing.Q3
S15 is the main one. Put the answer (the cause) in the first line, then "Use narrative only where it carries understanding - for instance when the path you took is the evidence". A "why" answer is one of the few places where some narrative may earn its place. M10 asks that the director end up seeing what you see, with "nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", and with depth (the rest of the causal chain) "available on request and never pushed". ST1 is the set that routes you to both, and S0 applies as the style entry point. A13 asks you to keep the answer judgement-shaped, not a dump. AR4's provenance tags ([MEASURED] vs [ARGUED], artifacts/AR4-decision-record.md) separate what was observed from what was reasoned, but AR4 governs decision records, not messages. NOT SETTLED: no entry in the Explain set tells you to mark which parts of a causal explanation were measured and which were inferred. GUESS: borrowing AR4's split would help.

### explain-routing.Q4
M10 is the main procedure, because its "Understand" limit covers this case. Take one confirmed step at a time, "establish the understanding first, and confirm it has landed", and keep depth available but never pushed. S15 sets the form of each message: point first, one step, and "Explain any system-specific name, label or ID the human has not seen, or leave it out", while general engineering terms may be assumed. Both are reached through ST1, and S0 applies as the style entry point. NOT SETTLED: no entry addresses getting the director ready to explain the concept to others. Its trigger, though, is documentation, not messages.

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame asks for "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". Each option becomes a "Continue" pick, and Explore/Return keep their meanings. M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message, with every real option and its cost, "none is hidden", and the director able to answer "with a pick, not a paragraph". Step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning, and to say where in one line. The deadline belongs in S15's "why now" (GUESS: no entry names deadlines directly). A13 says you must never rework or narrow the option set or pre-empt the decision, and any preference "lean" must be labelled and used only as a tie-break. AR4 and W23 are where the decision is recorded and ratified (M10 "When to apply"). R4 confirms that ratification is the director's alone. AR3 offers many moves at once, which ST1 flags as a tension with "one decision at a time". Possible conflict: S15 says to end with "two or three picks". Three options plus "doing nothing" plus Explore/Return comes to more than that, and NOT SETTLED.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main entry: "one decision at a time", one decision per message, and record each decision before moving on. Step 3 says to establish understanding first when a decision depends on it. GUESS: by the same logic, ask the first decision, record it, and then frame the second given that answer. The text covers decisions that depend on understanding, not decisions that depend on other decisions, so this is NOT SETTLED. S15 shapes each message: one question at the end, the decision frame, and "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line" (for example, decision 1 of 2). A13 asks you to economise attention at gate-points and keep the full option set. AR4/W23 record and ratify each ruling. AR4 also has an `amends`/`supersedes` field for rulings that change earlier ones. AR3 requires a "Decisions required" section in which each entry names "exactly what it blocks", which suits dependent decisions. ST1, however, flags AR3 as being in tension with one decision at a time. K5's two-round survey frames Round 2 on Round 1's answers, but ST1 notes its "three questions a round" conflicts with one question at a time, and its trigger is design intent capture.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", and also that each decision option is a Continue pick, with "doing nothing" required as an option and Explore/Return kept. A three-option decision cannot meet all of these at once.
- Possible tension: A13 says to engage the director "at gate-points rather than per-step", while M10/S15 say one step per message. They can probably be reconciled, but no entry does so.
- ST1's frontmatter `related` list omits W24, K5, AR3 and RU1, all of which its body names as related.
- S15 and M10 cite evidence at docs/evals/human/... and MATRIX.md, which are not in this corpus, so their evidence claims cannot be checked from it.
- No entry covers bad news, deadlines, "why" explanations, or decisions that depend on other decisions. ST1 itself records bad news and teaching as gaps.
- INDEX.md tells you to fetch from GitHub if no local checkout exists. I did not; I used only the provided corpus.
