### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is the router: its members for "the form of any message to a human" and "leading a human to understanding, then a decision" are S15 and M10. S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks that "the first line is the conclusion", so the slip goes first. It also asks for one question at the end, that you show the step and the finish line, that you explain or leave out names the director has not seen, and that you use the decision frame if you ask for anything. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks for one step per message and one decision per message, with "nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", and for the decision to be recorded. A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) applies because you are consuming director attention: "decision over status", but also "never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed", so you must surface the slip and must not bury it. S0 (style/README.md) has a matching trigger ("a message a human will read") and points you to S15. NOT SETTLED: no entry covers bad news specifically, and ST1 records "Bad news ... tested in one pair" as a gap.

### explain-routing.Q5
ST1 routes to S15 and M10. S15's decision frame: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". The deadline is the "why now". Each option becomes a Continue pick, and "A request for the human to act is never hidden in an optional pick". M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message with every real option and none hidden. Step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning, which points to AR4 (decision record) and W23 (capture and ratify). A13 says you may recommend but must never narrow "the option set" or "pre-empt the decision", and a preference lean may appear only as a "labeled tie-break input". AR3 (board) is listed in ST1 as related but in tension ("offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time"), so it matches only if the three options are next moves on a board.

### explain-routing.Q6
ST1 routes to S15 and M10. M10 is explicit: "one decision at a time", and "Ask for one decision per message". Step 3 says that where a decision depends on understanding, you establish and confirm it first. Step 5 says to record each decision where it will be found and say where, which points to AR4 and W23. So you ask the first decision, record it, then ask the second. S15 asks for "One step, one question, asked at the end", with the full decision frame for each decision and "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line". A13 forbids pre-empting the second decision based on the first. AR3 offers a related idea ("a move whose dependency is unmet is not legal yet"), but ST1 marks it as in tension with one decision at a time. NOT SETTLED: whether to show the second decision in advance; GUESS: M10 step 1 ("say how many steps remain") suggests you say that a second, dependent decision follows, without asking it yet.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", while its decision frame makes each option a Continue pick and requires "doing nothing" as an option. Three options plus doing nothing (Q5), plus Explore or Return, gives more than three picks, so the two instructions conflict.
- S5's trigger ("about to name a version, date ... in prose") would fire on a slipped release date (Q1, Q5), but the rule is scoped to "user-facing prose" docs. It is ambiguous whether it reaches a message to the director; GUESS: it does not.
- S6, S11 and S13 match by trigger whenever the message is markdown prose, but the corpus does not say whether a chat message counts as "markdown prose".
- A13's "Status-chatter ... never reach the Director" sits in tension with sending a progress update (Q2), and only the non-dark floor reconciles them.
- AGENTS.md and the INDEX preamble name no ID for the standing context, so it cannot be cited by ID.
- W24 says the director leads the walkthrough, but also "the Director themselves is talked through the state", so it is unclear who conducts it.
- ST1 names W24 as related in its prose, but W24 is missing from its `related:` frontmatter.
