### composition.P5
No, you cannot do it yourself.
W8's hydrate-when is "a build or change that you did not author", its falsifier includes "self-attestation", and R3 says the verifier "may never attest own executed work".
W0 constraint 2 says that if the roster collapses attester and executor to one agent you "fail the seed, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in".
Instead: (1) still check your own work, which R0 says is "always required" and "mandatory", using means your own reasoning cannot bias - a mechanism that fails when the work is wrong, a fresh no-context instance, a measurement rather than a re-read - for example W4 validate-locally, whose evidence is `executor-evidence-provisional` and does not count as assurance.

### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs.
W0 (work-types/README.md, Faults): "only the work-type's list governs, and the domain's view is generated"; D0: "The work-type owns the pairing ... Each domain entry shows the work-types that act on it, generated from the work-types' own lists, so the two cannot disagree"; each domain table says "Generated from each work-type's `domainEligibility`, the list that governs".
So a disagreement means the generated view is stale; regenerate it, do not edit it.
(b) The axiom's `applies-to` would govern.
A0 (axioms/README.md, "What brings an axiom into force"): "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does"; T0: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its `applies-to` field".
The corpus never addresses a trait-list-versus-applies-to conflict directly, so I derived this from A0's "only field" sentence; the trait lists are hand-written, not generated (see OBSERVATIONS).

### OBSERVATIONS
- The trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists (T1-T5) are hand-maintained, with no generated markers, and tools/check-traits.mjs checks only the applies-to enum against trait names, not these lists. This is the "doubly-declared" shape D0 and W0 name as a fault for domain/work-type pairings. They agree with applies-to today (I checked all 14 axioms), but nothing enforces it.
- W8's Evidence section says that if the roster collapses attester and executor, "the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`"; W0 constraint 2 says "a same-agent review never stands in" and constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". A same-agent downgrade to kind:review reads as contradicting the canonical set.
- R3's engagement-mode lists work-types (meta-validate-dogfood, code-owner-approve) whose domain or role lists do not line up with the verifier/product-data pairings; it is role prose, not a governing list, but a reader could take it as the verifier's menu.
- W4 validate-locally excludes product-data, so the corpus offers no self-check work-type on product-data before W7 applies a migration; D7's A9 requirement (sandbox proof, restore point) has no work-type carrying it.
- W7's title and hydrate-when ("ship to the estate/channel") do not mention applying data changes, though its domainEligibility includes product-data; the W7 classification for applying a migration is inferred rather than stated.
- INDEX.md line 24 and AGENTS.md point to a remote raw.githubusercontent.com address; I did not follow it, per the instruction to read only this corpus.
