### work-layers.L3
It belongs in the work-types layer (`work-types/`, prefix W). W0 says a candidate "is taxonomy-grade iff it compiles to a closeable claimable node". Its schema holds exactly the three parts asked for: `roleEligibility` (who), `domainEligibility` plus `parameters`/target (what it may be pointed at), and `evidenceContract`/`evidenceAuthority`/`falsifier` (what proof ends it) (work-types/README.md lines 148-167).
An entry already covers it: W10 (adversarial-design-review-upstream), "A verifier red-teams a design **before** build or merge, producing a critique doc that raises specific, falsifiable failure hypotheses" (W10 line 35).
- Who: `roleEligibility: [verifier]`, with `verifier-attestation`. The attester "cannot be the executor" (constraint 2).
- Pointed at: `target` = "the design under review", bound `provided-by-trigger`, in any of the seven domains (`free`).
- Proof that ends it: one `kind:review` critique doc. The falsifier is "review after the decision, or no falsifiable concerns raised".
W10 cites M1 as the method that conducts it. W0 warns against "Two work-types for one act" (line 230).

### work-layers.L4

### work-layers.L5
Where it goes depends on what form the guidance takes. Under M0's ladder, a procedure that produces a handover result would be a method at the "handing over" moment. Guidance that requires a handover record to carry something would be a rule. Guidance that leaves no trace would be a practice. RU0 names the rule-shaped candidate: "A rule for handing over - *a handover record names what is unfinished* - would leave a trace, and none exists" (rules/README.md line 43).
The corpus does record the absence. M0 says "handing work in progress to another agent or a later session has no procedure at all", and that "a lone agent hands over to its own next session". It adds: "Neither is a defect until something fails there, and both are where to look first when something does" (methods/README.md lines 47-50). PC0 says handing over holds "no practice today", and that a practice is not minted just to fill a moment (practices/README.md lines 40-41). M10 covers only handing understanding and decisions to a human.
The axiom A4 bears on it: "A cold pickup from the record alone reproduces the same outputs as a warm actor" (axioms/A4-zero-loss-knowledge.md line 50).
NOT SETTLED: which of the three layers the guidance would land in, because none has been written and the answer depends on its form.

### work-layers.L6
The corpus gives one test per layer:
- **Method:** "Does following it produce a result of its own - a verdict, an artifact, a record? A procedure, here" (methods/README.md line 72).
- **Skill:** "A skill is *invoked*, not followed: it names a procedure with inputs, steps and an output". Also: "A method is a procedure held as guidance ... a skill packages a procedure so an agent can install and invoke it, and earns its packaging where scripts or assets make it necessary" (skills/README.md lines 14-17).
- **Work-type:** "taxonomy-grade iff it compiles to a closeable claimable node", and a work-type "cites the procedure that conducts it, in `methods`" (work-types/README.md lines 148, 82).
Method versus skill is the hard pair. Both are procedures with outputs, and the only difference is packaging. M0's five-question placement ladder (lines 69-75) has no skill step at all, and M0 lists "The procedure that is really a skill" as a fault (line 117). This confusion has already happened: M9 history-content-scrub was filed as skill K1 and moved ("as `M9` was before it moved", skills/README.md line 52).
Method versus work-type is separated by citation: W8 and W10 cite M1, W14 and W22 cite M7, and W24 cites M10. Some names still read alike, for example M7 "axiom alignment audit" and W22 "axiom-alignment-gate".

### work-layers.L7
The entries below govern this sweep, grouped by step.

**Running the sweep and choosing what lands**
- RU1 (rules/; trigger: "running an improvement sweep"). Default-reject: a candidate is rejected unless it "delivers more value than it costs, measured concretely against the current state".
- M1 (methods/). If the two landings ship to production or upstream, you need at least four independent inputs before the verdict.
- W6 (work-types/). Landing needs an already-approved PR, the merge commit, and branch-protection or review-status evidence. Its falsifier is "branch not merged to canonical main".
- W0 (work-types/README.md). Its constraints apply: for example, "a same-agent review never stands in" for independence.
- GUESS: if the sweep is an adversarial sweep of a bounded surface, W9 also applies. It needs a scope statement, a findings table bound to refs, and attestation by a verifier who is not the executor.

**Cutting three**
- RU3 (rules/). Each cut records a concrete revival trigger at the moment of cutting, as a required field. A cut stays reopenable, and a revived item is re-triaged, not resumed.
- AR5 (artifacts/). The backlog holds the cut rows, each with id, finding, evidence, state and trigger. Rows are retired, never deleted, and closed in the same commit as the act (AR5 lines 43-67, 75-78).
- RU2 (rules/). These records are never rewritten later. If they diverge from current state, add a status banner and a cross-link instead.
- M0 confirms this grouping: "A procedure that cuts or defers anything is held by" RU1, RU3 and RU2 (methods/README.md lines 105-107).

**Reporting to the director**
- RU1 again. The report leads with yield before any narrative: 5 candidates considered, 2 landed, 3 cut, and what was verified versus only asserted.
- S15 (style/). Point first, explain any IDs the director has not seen, use the decision frame if you ask for a decision, and end with two or three picks.
- ST1 (sets/) groups S15 with M10 (methods/). M10 applies if the director must decide: one decision at a time, every option including doing nothing, and the decision recorded (AR4, W23).
- A13 (axioms/; applies to multi-agent and autonomous systems). Surface decisions, not status chatter. Never narrow the options. Never go dark on something the director is owed.
- R4 (roles/). Director ratification "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative".
- ST1 lists RU1 as a tension, because RU1 is "triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report". This report comes from a sweep, so RU1 applies, and its yield-first opening fits S15's "point first".
