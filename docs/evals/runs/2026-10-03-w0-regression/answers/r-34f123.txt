S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks: "Point first: the first line is the conclusion" (here, the slip itself); one step and one question, asked at the end; explain or leave out system-specific names; end with two or three Continue/Explore/Return picks. A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) triggers when you "consume the director's attention": it says "never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed - under-surfacing is as much a fault as over-surfacing" and "decision over status". NOT SETTLED: no entry covers bad news as such, and ST1 records this gap itself: "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair; no move is isolated". GUESS: S5 (do not pin dates in prose) is aimed at documentation drift, not at a message giving a new release date.

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame applies: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". The deadline is the "why now", and "doing nothing" is an extra option beside your three. M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message, with every option and its cost and none hidden ("checking that needs the real options"), so the director "can answer with a pick, not a paragraph". M10 step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning and give the location in one line. A13 says you may recommend but must never rework the option set, narrow the choices or pre-empt the decision, so present all three in full; R4 says the decide act is the director's alone. AR4 (decision record: ruling, refuted alternatives, costs, status) and W23 (capture and ratify, needs a director signal and not "architect narrative") govern the record that follows, as M10 "When to apply" says. ST1 marks K5 (three questions a round) and AR3 (many moves at once) as related but in tension with one-decision-at-a-time, so they are not guidance for this message. NOT SETTLED: S15's "two or three picks" are Continue/Explore/Return, and no entry says how three options plus doing nothing fit into those picks.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main entry: "one decision at a time" and "Ask for one decision per message", with step 1 asking you to "Know the finish line ... and say how many steps remain". Its step 3 says that if a decision depends on understanding, you establish it first and confirm it landed. S15 asks for one question per message, a line showing "the step, and the finish line" (for example "decision 1 of 2"), and the decision frame for each decision. A13 says "A single Director approval may execute an entire downstream chain", but the approval "is never synthesized on the Director's behalf", so do not pre-decide the second decision from the first. AR4 says each ruling must be "separately addressable, separately dated, and separately superseded", so record two rulings; W23 governs ratifying each one. AR3 treats dependent moves as a graph ("a move whose dependency is unmet is not legal yet"), but ST1 records its tension with one-decision-at-a-time. NOT SETTLED: no entry says how much to preview the second decision, or how its options depend on each first-decision answer, while you ask the first.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 and M10 cite their evidence as `docs/evals/human/runs/guidance-1-*` and `docs/evals/human/MATRIX.md`, and neither path exists in this corpus, so I could not check the "4 of 4" claims. ST1 admits this: the evaluation procedure "exists only as a tool and suites in this corpus's own records".
- S15 asks for picks "each starting with one word: Continue / Explore / Return", which are navigation moves, while its decision frame and M10 ask the director to choose among options "with a pick". It is unclear whether the options are the picks or sit beside them, and the clash is sharpest when there are 3 options plus doing nothing (Q5) and only two or three picks are allowed.
- A13's frontmatter has `applies-to: [multi-agent, autonomous]`, so whether it binds depends on traits of the system. ST1 and M10 still cite it as a source of the decide limit, which suggests it applies to any message to the director, but no text says so.
- S5's trigger ("name a version, date or other point-in-time identifier in prose") matches a slip message word for word. Its body and "When to apply" are about user-facing documentation, so the trigger is wider than the rule.
- S6 triggers on "markdown prose that someone else will read". It is not clear whether a chat message to a human counts.
- INDEX.md says links resolve against a mission-kit checkout or GitHub raw URLs. I used only this corpus copy, as instructed.
