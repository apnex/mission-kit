### charter-claims.V1
No. The territory should not be narrowed just because a part of it is empty. entities/E3-set.md: "Without a stated territory a gap is invisible", and entities/E4-charter.md requires that a territory "shows a gap or argues that there is none", because "A territory that names at least one real, checked absence has demonstrated that it can detect one". Charters do this in practice: methods/README.md records "Two thin moments, recorded rather than filled", and domains/README.md and work-types/README.md say the work "is recorded against the gap rather than forced into the nearest domain". The limit (E4): the territory must be "derived from the population, not imposed on it". So an empty cell is legitimate only if it is a real, checked absence, and a partition that no member's subject supports is suspect. Stating scope is also not all a charter does. E4 lists six required concerns (Purpose, Territory, Boundaries, Composition, Faults, Index), then conditional ones (Kinds, Member shape, Placement, Enforcement boundary, Sub-sets, Axiom alignment, Probes), then free sections.

### OBSERVATIONS
- practices/README.md "Gaps tested" lists three empty moments, but its own table shows five empty (committing to a design and treating the record are left out).
- axioms/README.md scopes out malice only for "collaborating agents"; hostile external input or actors are neither covered nor recorded as a gap, against E4's requirement to show gaps.
- artifacts/README.md says each type has "a schema", but no machine schema governs instances; "schema" there seems to mean a prose shape, which is ambiguous next to the schemas/ layer.
- domains/README.md "Seven subject-surfaces" and work-types/README.md "N=7" / "K~=26" are hard counts that sit uneasily with the README admission rule "No point-in-time content".
- roles/README.md territory covers only authorities within a unit of work. Corpus-level authorities (minting layers, ratifying work-types, choosing next work) are assigned to the director elsewhere but not placed in R0's territory, so R0's fault "The authority with no role ... records none today" holds only under its narrow scope.
- Revising or retiring a corpus entry has no work-type, and W0 records only the layer-level gap.
- AGENTS.md section 6 commit-hygiene guidance leaves a checkable trace but has no RU entry, which is the "rule with no entry" case M0's placement test would send to rules/.
- E3/E4 "Thirteen charters" count may be stale (INDEX lists more charter slots, e.g. ST0, PC0, RU0).
