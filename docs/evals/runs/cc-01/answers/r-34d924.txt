### OBSERVATIONS
- work-types/README.md, "Gaps recorded": two consecutive sentences contradict each other. The first says "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." The next says "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." This reads like an edit that was not reconciled.
- artifacts/README.md (AR0) opens by saying the document types come "each with a schema, so a document is instantiated rather than reinvented". schemas/README.md (SC0) says lifecycle documents have "no contract at all" and only prose templates. Either "schema" in AR0 means a prose template, or the two files disagree.
- work-types/README.md states "DOMAINS (N=7)" and "WORK-TYPES (K~=26)" as fixed counts. The domains and work-types charters both say their sets grow when a gap is found. If those counts are not updated, they will become stale.
- methods/README.md: the "---" after "A procedure that cuts or defers anything is held by them." has no blank line before it. In CommonMark that turns the line into a setext heading instead of a horizontal rule.
- README.md lists a `docs/` layer in the Mechanics table, but this corpus snapshot has no `docs/` directory. README.md also calls INDEX.md "the flat ledger", but INDEX.md is grouped by layer.
- entities/E3-set.md and E4-charter.md refer to "thirteen charters". The Mechanics table now lists sixteen ID-bearing layers. The number may be historical, but it is not marked as such.
