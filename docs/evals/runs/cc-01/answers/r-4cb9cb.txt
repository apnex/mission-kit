### OBSERVATIONS
- `practices/README.md` "Gaps tested" names three empty moments, but its own table shows five (committing to a design and treating the record are left out).
- `schemas/entry-body/v1alpha1/entry-body.json` requires these charter sections: "Territory", "What earns an entry", "Faults", "Index". `entities/E4-charter.md` says the fixed headings are Purpose, Territory, Faults, Index, and that admission (Boundaries) has a free heading. I grepped the charter headings: rules, practices, roles, domains, work-types, axioms and schemas have no "## What earns an entry" heading, and artifacts has no "## Territory" heading. I did not run `tools/check-entry-body.sh`, so I don't know whether charters are actually enforced.
- `entities/E4-charter.md` says "An exclusion without a route is half an answer". `axioms/README.md` excludes malice with no route.
- `roles/README.md` territory is per-unit-of-work, but `R4` claims curation and delegation authority, and A0/AR0 give the director cross-unit selection. The charter does not acknowledge that mismatch.
- `artifacts/README.md` says each type comes "with a schema", but only prose templates exist for project instances.
- Counts written in prose ("Seven subject-surfaces", "N=7", "K~=26", "thirteen charters") will go stale when the sets grow.
- `INDEX.md` and `AGENTS.md` point readers to remote fetches (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). I followed the prompt's constraint and did not fetch them.
