### charter-claims.V1
No, the charter should not narrow its claim just because no member fills part of it. E4 (entities/E4-charter.md, "A territory must earn itself") requires a territory to "show a gap or argue that there is none", and says "A territory that names at least one real, checked absence has demonstrated that it can detect one". E3 makes the same point: "A stated territory is what converts a miss into a finding", and an uncharted population "can be audited for validity and never for completeness". The territory must still be "derived from the population, not imposed on it", so every existing member has to land in exactly one place in it. The unfilled part should be recorded as a checked gap, as M0, RU0 and PC0 each do under "Gaps tested". Stating scope is not all a charter does. E4 requires Purpose, Territory, Boundaries, Composition, Faults and Index, and adds conditional concerns when they apply: Kinds, Member shape, Placement, Enforcement boundary, Sub-sets, Axiom alignment and Probes.

### OBSERVATIONS
- PC0 "Gaps tested" lists three moments with no practice, but its own table leaves five empty (it omits committing to a design and treating the record).
- E3 and E4 say "Thirteen charters in this corpus" in the present tense. INDEX.md lists 16 layer charters (`<prefix>0`) plus the set charter ST1, so I infer the count is stale.
- D0 Purpose ("Seven subject-surfaces") and W0 ("N=7", "K~=26") carry hard counts that conflict with the stated rule that these sets grow.
- R0's territory is scoped to positions within one unit of work, yet its fault "The authority with no role... records none today" reads as complete coverage. Cross-work authorities (board selection, delegation, ratifying a new work-type) sit outside its denominator and are held by R4 only through prose.
- W3 excludes knowledge-methodology, and W0's gap note covers only layers, so retiring or revising a corpus entry has neither a work-type nor a recorded gap.
- SC0's territory has no row, and no recorded gap, for project-written instances of AR types, even though AR3 says the board-record invariant "should be" mechanically checked.
- AR0 opens with "each with a schema", but the artifact types carry prose required sections, not machine schemas. The word "schema" is ambiguous next to SC0.
- A0 places malice out of scope inside a paragraph about a different gap, with no exclusion route as E4 requires for Boundaries ("An exclusion without a route is half an answer").
- M0, RU0 and PC0 cite "Delta-1" without defining it in the files I read.
- M0's Placement rules "credentials are never committed" to be a rule, but no RU entry exists for it.
