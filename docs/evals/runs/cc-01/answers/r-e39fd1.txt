### charter-claims.V1
No. The charter should not narrow its claim. entities/E4-charter.md says the vision "is what the set is for and what it claims to cover; the population is measured against it ... and it is never cut to fit what the set holds today". It also says "A claim no member yet fills is a gap - the distance between the population and its charter - and naming it is how gaps are found". The territory obligations say the same thing: "How far the territory reaches is set by what the set is for, not by what it holds: a cell no member fills is kept and named as a gap, and removing it to match the population erases the gap it exposes." Stating the scope is not all a charter does. E4 says "A charter holds the set's vision, and manages the set against it". The management part is the territory (where the population stands against the claim), the boundaries (what enters and where the rest goes), the faults, and changes made under "Changing a charter". The required concerns are Purpose, Territory, Boundaries, Composition, Faults and Index, and some sets also need conditional concerns such as Kinds, Member shape or Probes (E4 Discriminators; also entities/E3-set.md "A set states what a member cannot...").

### OBSERVATIONS
- work-types/README.md, Gaps recorded: two sentences next to each other disagree. One says "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." The next says "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." The first says the question is open and the second says it is settled.
- work-types/README.md Territory says the Build group "changes a system - the product, the harness, or the organisation's own machinery and knowledge". But the Gaps note, and the Build work-types' own domainEligibility lists, leave out the knowledge domain (and D4/D5). The group description claims more than its members cover.
- artifacts/README.md (AR0) opens by calling the document types "each with a schema". schemas/README.md (SC0) says lifecycle documents have no contract and "the artifact types carry prose templates". "Schema" is used loosely in AR0, or the two files disagree.
- work-types/README.md hard-codes "DOMAINS (N=7)" and "WORK-TYPES (K~=26)". These counts go stale whenever a domain or work-type is added, and D0 and W0 both say their sets are expected to grow.
- methods/README.md: a "---" rule comes straight after the "Rules hold what procedures record" paragraph with no blank line before it. In markdown that can turn the paragraph into a setext heading. It is minor.
- INDEX.md tells readers to fetch from a remote URL when there is no local checkout. These instructions limited me to the local corpus, so I did not fetch anything.
