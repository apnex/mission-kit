### work-layers.L1
It belongs in the rules layer (`rules/`, prefix `RU`): `methods/README.md` (M0) placement says guidance that produces nothing of its own and "leave[s] a trace in the record of the work that a check could test" is a rule, and it uses this exact case: "*every deferral records its revival trigger* is visible in text and is still a rule, because it governs what the work records."
It is already covered by `rules/RU3-anti-amnesia-deferral.md` (RU3), triggered "You are parking, cutting or marking won't-do on a unit of tracked work", which requires "a concrete **revival trigger**: the observable condition under which the item should be reconsidered" as "a required field, not an optional note", with `trace: every deferred, parked or cut item's record carries a revival trigger`.
`rules/README.md` places RU3 at the "Deciding what to keep" moment.
It was formerly `methods/M5-anti-amnesia-deferral.md`, now superseded ("Moved to RU3 by Delta-1").
The document that holds such rows is `artifacts/AR5-backlog.md` (AR5), whose "governing rule is RU3"; it says "'Later' is not a trigger" and rejects a trigger that is "a date or the word 'later'".
`backlog/README.md` (MREQ-0) applies the same requirement to corpus-level deferrals.

### work-layers.L2
Skills (`skills/`, prefix `K`).
`skills/README.md` (K0): a skill "is *invoked*, not followed: it names a procedure with inputs, steps and an output, and it is expected to be executed largely as written", and it earns an entry "when it is executable, repeatable, and would otherwise be reconstructed from scratch".
M0's boundary table agrees: "a capability you execute" goes to `skills/` because "the object is a procedure with inputs and outputs, invoked rather than followed", and M0 names "The procedure that is really a skill" as a fault.
Something very close already exists: `skills/K3-substrate-audit.md` (K3) runs "a **five-pass meta-process**" (Sweep, Triangulation, Promotion audit, Synthesis, Reconciliation) and is "structured around a fixed artefact layout" (`00-summary.md`, `02-architecture.md`, ... `sources.md`), with its body at `skills/substrate-audit/`.
Per K0 it needs a `K*` stub plus a portable `SKILL.md` body, and any steps a script could do should be that script (A11).
GUESS: M0's step-2 test ("produces a result of its own... an artifact") would also fit a method, so the deciding factor is "invoked rather than followed" plus the fixed inputs and outputs, not the fact that it produces output.

### work-layers.L3
Work-types (`work-types/`, prefix `W`): `README.md` says this layer owns "Units of work and their evidence contracts", and W0's entry schema carries `roleEligibility` (who), `domainEligibility`/`parameters.target` (what it may be pointed at), and `evidenceContract`/`evidenceAuthority`/`falsifier` (what proof closes it).
It is already covered by `work-types/W10-adversarial-design-review-upstream.md` (W10): "A verifier red-teams a design **before** build or merge, producing a critique doc that raises specific, falsifiable failure hypotheses".
Who: `roleEligibility: [verifier]`, with `verifier-attestation` requiring an attester who "**cannot be the executor**" given the live roster (constraint 2), falling back to plain `kind:review` if the roster collapses.
Target: `target` = "the design under review", `bindingSource: provided-by-trigger`, across all seven domains (`domainFreedom: free`).
Proof: a single `kind: review`, a "critique doc before build/merge with specific failure hypotheses"; falsifier: "review after the decision, or no falsifiable concerns raised".
The nine canonical closeability constraints live in `work-types/README.md` and are "satisfied here, not restated".

### work-layers.L4
It is a methodology entry, and it already exists: `methods/M1-triangulated-review.md` (M1), "require **at least four genuinely independent inputs** before reaching a verdict", triggered "You are reviewing a patch or design that ships to production or upstream".
By M0 placement step 2, following it "produce[s] a result of its own - a verdict", so it is a procedure, and M0 calls M1 the set's one primitive: "independent inputs before a verdict".
M1's inputs are upstream baseline, prior-version intent and review, archaeology, and community signal, "none of which were produced by the reviewer in the loop being audited".
It is not a skill (K0: a skill is "invoked, not followed" with fixed inputs and outputs), and it is not a work-type, because it carries no `roleEligibility`/`evidenceContract`/`falsifier`.
Related work-types can carry it: W8 `verify-gate-reactive`, W13 `code-owner-approve` ("Author != approver", W0 constraint 8) and W10 enforce reviewer independence structurally.
GUESS: "never trust a single reviewer" in the sense of separate people is held by those W0 independence constraints, while M1's independence is about the inputs, not the number of reviewers.

### work-layers.L5
It would sit at the "handing over" moment, one of the six moments M0 defines ("passing work in progress to another agent or a later session").
Which layer depends on its shape, per M0 placement: a procedure producing a record goes in `methods/`, a traceable rule in `rules/`, an untraceable habit in `practices/`.
The corpus records the absence explicitly in all three charters.
`methods/README.md`: "*Handing over* ... has no procedure at all", "handover is the moment that condition bites hardest", and "a lone agent hands over to its own next session", yet "Neither is a defect until something fails there".
`rules/README.md`: "A rule for handing over - *a handover record names what is unfinished* - would leave a trace, and none exists."
`practices/README.md`: "handing over hold[s] no practice today", and "A practice is not minted to fill a moment".
NOT SETTLED which layer a real entry would go in, since no MREQ backlog entry exists for it; adjacent text includes `traits/T1-stateful.md` (notes for a next session are persistent state) and `traits/T3-multi-agent.md` (handing to your own next session is "one actor across time, not two").

### work-layers.L6

### work-layers.L7
`rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md` (rules; trigger "running an improvement sweep"): default is reject, and you land only what "delivers more value than it costs, measured concretely against the current state"; the director report must lead with yield ("candidates considered, landed, and verified against asserted") "before any narrative" (its `trace`), e.g. 5 considered, 2 landed, 3 rejected.
