### explain-routing.Q1
Main entries: ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md), the "Explain" set, whose members are S15 and M10 ("members: [S15, M10]"). S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) sets the form. "Point first: the first line is the conclusion", so the slip goes in the first line. It also asks for "One step, one question, asked at the end", says to explain or drop system-specific names, and says to end with Continue/Explore/Return picks. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks you to know the finish line, give one step per message, and if a decision is needed, give "one decision at a time" with every option costed, "including doing nothing". A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) is cited by ST1 and M10. It says the system must "never go silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed", and "under-surfacing is as much a fault as over-surfacing". The root AGENTS.md has no ID, so it is not an entry, but it adds: "A caveat does not repair a headline", and say whether each claim was "measured or inferred". NOT SETTLED: no entry is written for bad news itself; ST1 records "Bad news ... tested in one pair" as a gap.

### explain-routing.Q2
ST1 is the set to start from. Its hydrate-when, "communicate with a human who has limited context", fits a director who has not followed the work. S15 says lead with the conclusion, "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line", and explain or leave out unfamiliar names. It also says "Leave out what you did when it does not change what the human understands or decides". M10 "When to apply" says "especially when they have not followed the work". It asks you to reach understanding "one confirmed step at a time, with depth available on request and never pushed". A13 cuts both ways. "Status-chatter ... never reach the Director", and what reaches them should be "decision over status", "digest over dump". But going silently dark on something the Director is owed is a fault. S0 (style/README.md) routes "a message a human will read" to S15. GUESS: RU1's "lead with yield" (rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md) fits in spirit, but ST1 says RU1 "is triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report", so it applies only if the update closes out a sweep.

### explain-routing.Q3
ST1 applies, and so do both of its members. S15 says put the conclusion (the cause) first. It also says "Use narrative only where it carries understanding - for instance when the path you took is the evidence", which is the case S15 names for a "why" answer. M10's "Understand" limit asks that the director "ends up seeing what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", with depth "available on request and never pushed". M10 also derives this from A5 ("the human sees what the agent sees") and A4 ("depth is kept rather than deleted"). The root AGENTS.md is standing context with no ID. It adds "Source settles mechanism; behaviour only shows that something is wrong", the measured-or-inferred labelling, and "Separate observation from explanation when sending". NOT SETTLED: no entry is written specifically for explaining causes.

### explain-routing.Q4
ST1 and M10 apply. M10's Understand limit asks for understanding "reached one confirmed step at a time". Step 1 asks you to say the finish line and "how many steps remain". Step 2 asks for one step per message with a pause point. Step 3 asks you to confirm understanding "has landed". S15 asks you to explain any system-specific name the director has not seen or leave it out ("General engineering terms may be assumed"). It also says to keep references in one line, show the step and the finish line, and offer an Explore pick for more depth. W24 (work-types/W24-director-walkthrough.md) cites M10, but it applies only to a live walkthrough the director leads. NOT SETTLED: the corpus does not cover helping the director retell the concept to others. ST1 says teaching was "tested in one pair" and has no isolated move. GUESS: K28 (ASD-STE100) is for documentation for non-native readers and does not apply here.

### explain-routing.Q5
ST1, S15 and M10 apply, and A13 adds a constraint. S15's decision frame asks for: "what is being decided; why now" (the deadline goes here); the options, "each with its cost, including doing nothing"; "a recommendation, labelled and kept separate"; then a pick. When you ask for a decision, "each option is a Continue pick naming it". M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message, with every real option shown and "none is hidden". Step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning, and say where in one line. A13 says you "never rework the option set, narrow the choices, nor pre-empt the decision". It also says any lean must be shown only as a "labeled tie-break input". AR4 and W23 (cited by M10 and ST1) govern how the chosen decision is recorded and ratified. NOT SETTLED: no entry says what to do if the deadline passes with no answer.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main entry. It asks for "one decision at a time" and "one decision per message" (step 4). So ask the first decision, then the second in a later message. Step 3 says that where a decision depends on understanding, you establish that understanding first and confirm it. Step 1 says to state the finish line and how many steps remain, so say up front that a second decision follows. S15 asks for "One step, one question, asked at the end", shown with the step and the finish line. ST1 notes that AR3 (a board offering "many moves at once") is in tension with "one decision at a time" and is not a member. AR4 asks that each ruling be "separately addressable"; its `amends`/`supersedes` fields handle a later ruling that changes an earlier one. NOT SETTLED: the corpus does not say how to show the second decision's options while they still depend on the first answer. GUESS: name the second decision and say it depends on the first, but do not frame its options until the first is answered.

### OBSERVATIONS
- Conflict: S15 says to end with "two or three picks", and when you ask for a decision, "each option is a Continue pick" while "Explore and Return keep their meaning". A three-option decision (Q5) therefore needs at least four picks if Explore or Return is kept. Doing nothing is a fourth costed option, which makes more. The rule does not say how to resolve this.
- Tension: A13 bars "status-chatter" from reaching the Director but also forbids going "silently dark". Neither A13 nor ST1 says where a progress update for a director who has not followed the work (Q2) falls.
- ST1 lists its own gaps: bad news and teaching were "each tested in one pair", and "no move is isolated". So Q1 and Q4 rest on guidance as a whole that is thinly tested.
- The evidence that S15 and M10 cite (`docs/evals/human/runs/guidance-1-*`, `docs/evals/human/MATRIX.md`) is not in this corpus, so I could not check it.
- W24 is "not an agent-executable task" (roleEligibility: director) but cites M10 as the procedure that conducts it. It is unclear who is meant to run M10 in a W24.
- INDEX.md tells you to resolve links against a checkout or GitHub. I used only the local corpus copy.
- The root AGENTS.md has relevant rules (measured or inferred, caveat versus headline) but no entry ID, so it cannot be cited as an entry.
