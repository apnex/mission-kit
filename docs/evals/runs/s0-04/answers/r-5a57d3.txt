### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) routes here ("You are about to communicate with a human who has limited context") and names its two members, S15 and M10. S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks for "Point first: the first line is the conclusion", so the slip goes in line one. It also asks you to explain or drop any system-specific names, to "Show where you are", to end with one question and Continue/Explore/Return picks, and to use the decision frame if you are asking for something. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks that the director end up "seeing what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", one confirmed step at a time. A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) routes on "about to consume the director's attention". It says "the system never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed", and it puts decision over status. S0 (style/README.md) routes on "a message a human will read" and points you to S15. No entry is specific to bad news: ST1 records "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair; no move is isolated", so that part is NOT SETTLED. AGENTS.md section 5.1 ("A caveat does not repair a headline") also applies, but it has no entry ID.

### explain-routing.Q2
M10 applies "especially when they have not followed the work" (M10, When to apply). It asks you to know the finish line, give one step per message, and keep depth "available on request and never pushed". S15 asks you to put the conclusion first and to explain names the director has not seen. It also says: "Leave out what you did when it does not change what the human understands or decides." ST1 is the set that routes you to both. S0 routes on writing a message a human will read. A13 warns against "Attention Leak" and "status-chatter". It wants "digest over dump, decision over status", so the update should carry only what the director needs to judge. RU1 (rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md) asks you to lead with the delivered outcome, but only for "triage / improvement-sweep / review" summaries. ST1 records that as a tension ("triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report"), so whether RU1 applies to a general progress update is NOT SETTLED. GUESS: leading with the outcome fits S15's "point first" anyway.

### explain-routing.Q3
S15 asks for the conclusion first (the cause), one question at the end, and names explained. It also says "Use narrative only where it carries understanding - for instance when the path you took is the evidence". That is the case where a "why" answer may tell what happened. M10's "understand" limit asks that the director sees what you see, with depth offered rather than pushed. ST1 routes to both, and S0 routes to S15. A13 applies because you are spending the director's attention: keep it to what they need. No entry settles how to mark certainty in a message. AR4 provenance tags ([MEASURED]/[ARGUED]) are for decision records, not messages, so that is NOT SETTLED by an entry. AGENTS.md sections 5.1/5.2 ("Measured or inferred", "Source settles mechanism") would apply, but they carry no entry ID. GUESS: say which parts of the cause you measured and which you inferred.

### explain-routing.Q4
M10 is the procedure for when "you need a human to understand something". It asks you to reach the "understand" limit one confirmed step at a time, to say how many steps remain, and to confirm each step "has landed". S15 sets the form of each message: point first, one step and one question, unfamiliar names explained or left out ("General engineering terms may be assumed"), and picks at the end. Explore is the way to offer more depth. ST1 routes to both, and S0 routes to S15. A13 applies to spending the director's attention. No entry deals with preparing the director to describe something to others. ST1 also records that teaching "was tested in one pair", so that goal is NOT SETTLED. K28 (ASD-STE100) routes only on documentation "readable by a non-native English speaker", so it does not apply unless that is true. GUESS: confirming understanding (M10 step 3) is the nearest check that they could re-explain it.

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame asks for: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". The deadline is the "why now", and missing it is the "doing nothing" option. S15 also says "each option is a Continue pick naming it". M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message with "none is hidden". Its step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning, and to say where in one line. A13 says never to rework or narrow the option set or pre-empt the decision. A lean may appear only "as a labeled tie-break input". R4 (roles/R4-director.md) and W23 (work-types/W23-capture-decision-and-ratify.md) say ratification is the director's alone. W23 and AR4 (artifacts/AR4-decision-record.md) are where the decision is captured and recorded, with status, authority and refuted alternatives. ST1 notes a tension with K5 survey ("three questions a round"), but that concerns questions, not options. What happens if the deadline passes with no answer is NOT SETTLED.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 says "one decision at a time" and "Ask for one decision per message". So ask the first decision, record it, and only then ask the second. M10 step 1 asks you to "say how many steps remain". S15 says "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line". Together these let the director know a second, dependent decision follows. M10 step 3 asks you to establish and confirm any understanding a decision depends on before asking it. Each decision uses S15's decision frame and is recorded per M10 step 5 in an AR4 record through W23. A13 forbids narrowing the second decision's options before the director has made the first. ST1 records a tension with AR3, whose board "offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time". It is NOT SETTLED whether the first message should preview how each first-decision option changes the second decision's options. GUESS: name that dependency in the first decision's option costs, since M10 asks that each option's cost be known.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", but also says that for a decision "each option is a Continue pick naming it, and Explore and Return keep their meaning". With three options that gives three Continue picks plus Explore/Return, so up to five. The two statements conflict.
- A13 says "status-chatter" never reaches the director and prefers "decision over status". The corpus has no rule for a progress update the director needs, so A13 and S15/M10 do not fully agree for Q2.
- ST1's body names W24, K5, AR3 and RU1 as related, but its frontmatter `related` list leaves them out.
- A13 has `applies-to: [multi-agent, autonomous]`, so whether it binds depends on the system's traits (T0). The questions do not state those traits.
- The evidence cited by S15 and M10 (`docs/evals/human/...`) is outside this corpus, so I could not check it.
- The claim-discipline rules that matter for Q1/Q3 (caveat vs headline, measured vs inferred) are only in AGENTS.md and have no entry ID. An agent routing by INDEX.md would not reach them.
- ST1 itself records the gaps: bad news and teaching were tested in only one pair each, and no single move has been tested alone.
