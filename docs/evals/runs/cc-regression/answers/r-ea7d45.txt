### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. domains/README.md: "Which domains a work-type may act on is declared on the work-type... Each domain entry shows the work-types that act on it, generated from the work-types' own lists, so the two cannot disagree."
W0 Faults: "only the work-type's list governs, and the domain's view is generated". D1 and D7 also say: "Generated from each work-type's `domainEligibility`, the list that governs."
(b) The axiom's applies-to field governs. axioms/README.md: "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does". traits/README.md: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its applies-to field".
The corpus does not state a tie-break for this disagreement in so many words. "The only field that does" settles it by implication.
Unlike the domain views, the trait lists are not generated, and tools/check-traits.mjs checks only that the applies-to vocabulary matches the traits. So such a disagreement is possible today without any check failing.

### OBSERVATIONS
- Trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists are hand-maintained copies of the axioms' applies-to fields. They are not generated and no check covers them. This is the "doubly-declared" shape that domains/README.md and W0 name as a fault for pairings. They agree today: I checked all 14 axioms against T1 to T5.
- W8 body: "if the live roster collapses attester and executor, the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`". This can be read as allowing a same-agent review. W0 constraints 2 and 9 and roles/README.md say "a same-agent review never stands in". The words "downgrades to kind:review" are ambiguous.
- W0 "Gaps recorded" has two contradictory consecutive sentences: "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." then "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." This reads like an edit left half-applied.
- R3's engagement-mode list (W8, W9, W10, W11, W12, W13) does not match the work-types whose roleEligibility includes verifier. Those also include W16, W20, W22 and W25, and the list omits all four. No text says whether role prose or roleEligibility governs. I assumed roleEligibility governs.
- No work-type explicitly covers applying a migration to live records. W7 is titled "ship to the estate/channel", but D7 assigns the estate to distribution. D7 itself only names W7 by analogy.
- INDEX.md tells readers to resolve links against a GitHub URL. The task restricted me to the local corpus, so I did not follow that.
