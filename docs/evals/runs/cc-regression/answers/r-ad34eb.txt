### w0.Q1
The closeability preflight is the seed-time projection of the nine-constraint canonical set in work-types/README.md (W0): "The `closeabilityPreflight` of a work-type is exactly the seed-time projection of this set - a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them."
A generated node that cannot pass it at seed "is not admitted", which W0 says is what makes the catalog safe for autonomous generation (A7, A8).
The nine constraints cover: permissive eligibility with strict authority, roster-aware structural independence, domain eligibility, required targetRef/references, a required falsifier, a repair path for completion-gated seeds, generation-mode gating of the idle pool, author != approver, and bounded degradation.
No, an entry may not restate them: W0 Purpose says "the constraint set is authored here once and never forked per entry", the body-section schema says "`## Evidence & closeability` (reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it)", and the Faults list names "The restated constraint" (the copy drifts from the one source).
Entries follow this, e.g. W22 and W23 say "do not restate the constraint set here".
An entry may still name which constraint is load-bearing for it (W23 cites constraint 8; W22 cites constraint 2).

### w0.Q3
`domainFreedom` is a work-type field (W0 entry schema: `domainFreedom: free | pinned`).
Free: object-level build/ship/assurance work acts on a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice the generator may vary within the type's `domainEligibility` (D0 "Bimodal freedom"; e.g. W9 audit can target any of the seven domains).
Pinned: meta/substrate work "whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it" (D0); W0 constraint 3: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and the schema says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".
D0 says pinned work lands on the substrate domains D4, D5, D6 (e.g. W22/W23 pinned to authority-governance, W18-W21 to coordination-substrate, W16 to knowledge-methodology).
W0 states the consequence honestly: the "aggregate > sum" claim holds fully for the free third and is "honestly `pinned`" for the meta third.
Freedom lives on the work-type, not the domain, because D4/D5/D6 remain valid free targets for other types (D0).

### w0.Q4
No - not even marked as weaker.
W0 constraint 2: if the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent, "fail the seed, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."
Constraint 9: in a thin roster degradation is ONLY "defer-until-independent-seat" or "director-ratification", never to the architect if the architect is or could be the executor, and "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation."

The only "weaker" status the corpus provides is `executor-evidence-provisional` (constraint 7), whose closure "does not count as assurance until an independent gate consumes it" - that is a type's evidenceAuthority for idle-pooled executor work, not a substitute for a required verifier attestation.

### w0.Q5
W0 "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in":
1. The systems the change alters give the traits and so the axioms in force (via E5 and T0); the organisation doing the work is also a system whose axioms govern how the work is done and recorded.
2. The work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence (work-types/W*.md frontmatter).
3. The domain is the surface the work's evidence lands on (D0; must be in the work-type's `domainEligibility`).
4. The role is who performs it; what it may attest is set by role and work-type together (R0).
5. The method and artifact are those the work-type cites in its `methods` and `produces` fields.
W0 stresses these attach "by citation or by system, never by widening the triple" (role x work-type x domain).

### w0.Q7
Per W0 Territory: close the gap by adding a new work-type, "not by stretching a neighbour to cover it, because a stretched work-type carries two evidence contracts under one name."
Until it exists, the work is recorded against the gap as a row in the project's backlog (AR5).
This applies only if the work "compiles to a closeable claimable node"; if it cannot (no trigger, nothing to close) it is recorded as a routing note instead, like incident recovery.
The new work-type is authored from a peer exemplar (PC1), against W0's entry schema, landing through the gate, and - because it changes how work is generated - is ratified by the director.

### w0.Q8
W0 "Faults" lists the population faults, visible across the set but invisible in any one entry:
- Two work-types for one act: the same node mintable under two names.
- The restated constraint: an entry copies the canonical set instead of satisfying it, and the copy drifts.
- The doubly-declared pairing: domain pairing stated on both the work-type and the domain, free to disagree (only the work-type's list governs; D0 records this once disagreed in 23 places).
- The uncovered work: a recurring kind of work no work-type covers, so it is hand-routed every time.
- The uncited procedure: a method cited by no work-type, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
GUESS: by W0's Territory, a stretched work-type carrying two evidence contracts would also be unhealthy, though it is not listed under Faults.
