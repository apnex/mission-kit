- S15 says "End with two or three picks", but it also says that for a decision "each option is a Continue pick" and Explore and Return keep their meaning. With three options (Q5), that gives up to five picks, which contradicts "two or three".
- A13 says "Status-chatter ... never reach[es] the Director", which pulls against sending a progress update (Q2). Its own non-dark floor pulls the other way, and no entry reconciles the two.

- ST1 says W24 is "director-led". W24's text says the Director "is talked through the state", and M10 says W24 "cites this procedure as the one that conducts it". So who leads the walkthrough is ambiguous.
- K5 (three questions per round) and AR3 (many moves at once) conflict with M10's "one decision at a time". ST1 acknowledges this but leaves it unreconciled.
- S6's trigger ("markdown prose that someone else will read") could be read to cover a markdown message, but S0 files S6 under the Document medium and S15 as the only rule for messages, so whether S6 applies is ambiguous.
- Bad news (Q1) and teaching (Q4) have no dedicated guidance, which ST1 records as gaps.
