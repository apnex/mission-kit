### composition.P1
Thirteen axioms bind it: A1, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13, A14 - every axiom except A2. Method: axioms/README.md (A0) says "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does", that an axiom binds "if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies **at least one** of the axiom's `applies-to` tags", and traits/README.md (T0) says "a system's obligations are the union of every axiom any of its traits brings into force, plus every axiom on the floor". I applied the trait tests: T1 stateful (persistent state it reads back), T3 multi-agent (work crosses between cooperating agents), T5 llm-in-the-loop (the model decides on the critical path); T2 declarative does not hold, since nothing reconciles to a declaration. I then read each axiom's `applies-to` frontmatter: any-system gives A3, A4, A8, A9, A14; stateful gives A1; multi-agent gives A5, A6, A7, A10, A13; llm-in-the-loop gives A5, A11, A12; only A2 (`applies-to: [declarative]`) is left out. Autonomy (T4) is not stated, but it would add nothing, because its axioms (A7, A10, A13) already bind through multi-agent. The trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists and the A0 applicability matrix agree with the frontmatter, but I treated the frontmatter as the deciding source. Per A0, "binds" means the asymptote rule: no change may move the system away from the limit, and each change moves it toward the limit as far as its scope allows.

### composition.P2
Only the five floor axioms bind it: A3, A4, A8, A9, A14 (each `applies-to: [any-system]`). It has no trait: T1 lists "a pure function" as not stateful, T2 lists "a script run once" as not declarative, T3 needs two independent actors (one person is one), and T5 needs a model on the critical path. A0 says "a plain library takes `any-system`", and E5 says "A one-shot script with no traits is a system, bound by the axioms every system carries". GUESS: I take it to be run interactively, so it is not T4 autonomous; if it were, A7, A10 and A13 would also bind. On A1: no, A1 does not constrain work on this formatter's codebase, because A1 is `applies-to: [stateful]`, A0 says `applies-to` is "the only field" that decides binding, and the domain only decides "what it demands on that surface" (A0, D0); D1's citation of A1 does not by itself bring A1 into force. E5 states the residual case directly: "A stateless product built by a team that keeps records it relies on has `A1` binding the team's records, not the product". So A1 may govern how your organisation records the work (W0 step 1), but not the formatter.

No. W8's trigger is "gating a build or change that you did not author", its falsifier includes "self-attestation", and R0 says "The same identity may not be both the executor and the independent verifier of one piece of work". ### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. W0 Faults says: "only the work-type's list governs, and the domain's view is generated". D0 "How domains compose" says: "The work-type owns the pairing ... Each domain entry shows the work-types that act on it, generated from the work-types' own lists, so the two cannot disagree". Each domain entry (for example D7, D1, D3) heads its list "Generated from each work-type's `domainEligibility`, the list that governs". (b) The axiom's `applies-to` field governs. A0 says: "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does"; T0 says "An axiom names the traits it governs in its `applies-to` field". No text states a precedence rule for this conflict by name, and unlike the domain lists, the trait lists are not marked as generated, so (b) is settled by A0's "only field" sentence rather than by an explicit conflict rule.

### OBSERVATIONS
- The trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists are a second, hand-written copy of the axioms' `applies-to`, which is the "doubly-declared" shape that W0 and D0 name as a fault. tools/check-traits.mjs only checks the enum against the trait names, not these lists. The lists agree today (I checked T1 to T5 against every axiom's frontmatter), but nothing appears to enforce that.
- W0 "Gaps recorded" contradicts itself in consecutive sentences: "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." then "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which."
- There is a tension between W8 and W0. W8 says that if the roster collapses attester and executor, "the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`". W0 constraints 2 and 9 say "a same-agent review never stands in" and "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". The W8 wording could be read as allowing a same-agent review.
- W7's definition and body talk only about release artifacts, the estate and channels, and never mention applying data or migrations. Its pairing with product-data rests on its `domainEligibility` and on D7's prose, so a reader of W7 alone would not know it covers migrations.
- D3's subjectSurface names specific tools ("ois/pi/claude launchers"). D0 acknowledges this as the "domain named after a tool" fault.
- A0 calls the matrix "Axioms grouped by `applies-to` tag", but it is not marked as generated, so it is another derived copy that could drift from the frontmatter.
