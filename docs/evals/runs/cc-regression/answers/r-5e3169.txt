### work-layers.L3
Work-types (`work-types/`, prefix W). A work-type pins down who (`roleEligibility`), what it may be pointed at (`domainEligibility`, `parameters`/`target`), the proof (`evidenceContract`, `evidenceAuthority`) and what fails it (`falsifier`). `work-types/README.md` (W0) states the test: "A candidate is taxonomy-grade **iff it compiles to a closeable claimable node**".
It is already covered by `work-types/W10-adversarial-design-review-upstream.md`: "A verifier red-teams a design **before** build or merge, producing a critique doc that raises specific, falsifiable failure hypotheses".
Who: `roleEligibility: [verifier]` with `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`, and the attester "cannot be the executor". What it may be pointed at: `target` = "the design under review", and all seven domains, `domainFreedom: free`.
What ends it: `evidenceContract` kind `review`, a "critique doc before build/merge with specific failure hypotheses". The falsifier is "review after the decision, or no falsifiable concerns raised".
It cites `methods: [M1]` as the procedure that conducts it.

### work-layers.L4
It is a methodology entry, and it already exists. `methods/M1-triangulated-review.md` says "require **at least four genuinely independent inputs** before reaching a verdict", and it triggers when "You are reviewing a patch or design that ships to production or upstream".
It is a method, not a rule, because it produces a result of its own (a verdict). `methods/README.md` question 2 asks: "Does following it produce a result of its own - a verdict, an artifact, a record? A procedure, here." M0 also calls M1 "the independence the verifying procedures specialise".

### work-layers.L7
Rules (`rules/`) govern the keep/cut decision. `RU1` sets "default disposition for a candidate is *reject*": land only what "delivers more value than it costs, measured concretely against the current state". Your report must lead with "how many candidates considered, how many landed, ... what was verified vs. asserted", before any narrative. `RU3` requires each of the three cuts to record, at that moment, an observable revival trigger, as a required field. Revival "re-triages, it does not resume".
Artifacts: the cuts become rows in an `AR5` backlog (Retired: "reason cut | revives on"), with evidence cited. They are retired, not deleted, and closed "in the same commit as the work that closed it". If a board (`AR3`, selected by the director) exists, deferred items go under `Held` and are scored, never "dropped without its reason being written back as a revival trigger". `RU2` forbids rewriting the sweep's records later: use a banner plus a cross-link.
Methods: `M0` says "Deciding what to keep" holds no procedure: "no method produces the verdict itself". If the landed changes ship to production, `M1` (four independent inputs) applies, conducted as work-type `W8`. Landing an approved change is work-type `W6` (merge commit plus review status). Note that `W3` deletes a surface; it is not for cutting candidates.
Reporting to the director: style `S15` puts the conclusion first, explains IDs, and uses a decision frame with picks. Method `M10` applies if you ask for a decision (record it per `W23`/`AR4`). Both are gathered by set `ST1`.
Axiom `A13` (applies-to multi-agent, autonomous) bars status-chatter and narrowing the option set, and forbids going "silently dark" on owed decisions. Role `R4` says ratification "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative".
