### w0.Q1
A work-type's `closeabilityPreflight` is "exactly the seed-time projection" of the nine-constraint canonical set in `work-types/README.md` (W0, section "The canonical constraint set (= the closeability preflight)"). A generated node that cannot pass it at seed "is not admitted", which is what makes the catalogue safe for autonomous generation (W0, citing A7, A8). No, an entry may not restate the constraints: "a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them", and the set "is authored here once and never forked per entry" (W0 Purpose). The schema's body-section rule says `## Evidence & closeability` must "reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it", and W0 Faults names "The restated constraint" as a population fault because "the copy drifts from the one source". An entry may still cite a particular constraint as load-bearing. For example, W23 cites "constraint 8", and W22 says "do not restate the constraint set here".

### w0.Q2
No. W0 section "Not a work-type: `recover-incident`" says incident recovery "is **not a generatable work-type**". It gives two reasons: "there is no `incident` substrate entity for a trigger to fire on", and "once an incident is machine-visible it *is* a bug". ### w0.Q3
Pinned is for "meta/substrate work whose *type names its own surface*". There "the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it", and such work lands on the substrate domains D4, D5 and D6 (D0, "Bimodal freedom"). Free is for object-level build/ship/assurance work that "*acts on* a surface". There the domain is "a free, discriminating choice", for example `verifier x audit-surface x distribution` versus `... x authority-governance` (D0; W0 "Two structural concepts"). Constraint 3 in W0 says "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain". The schema says `domainEligibility` holds "a single value when pinned" (W0 entry schema). W22, for example, is `domainFreedom: pinned` with `domainEligibility: [authority-governance]`. `domainFreedom` belongs to the work-type, not the domain (D0). W0 admits that the `aggregate > sum` claim is only "honestly `pinned`" for the meta third.

### w0.Q4

Constraint 9 limits a thin-roster check to two ways out, "**defer-until-independent-seat** or **director-ratification** ONLY". It adds: "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation." ### w0.Q5
W0, "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in", gives five steps. (1) The systems the change alters give the traits, and so the axioms in force (via E5 and T0). The organisation doing the work is also a system, and its axioms govern how the work is done and recorded. (2) The work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence. (3) The domain is the surface the work's evidence lands on (D0). (4) The role is who performs it, and what it may attest is set by role and work-type together (R0). (5) The method and artifact are the ones the work-type cites, in its `methods` and `produces` fields. W0 explains why: "Everything else attaches by citation or by system, never by widening the triple". Adding method or system as a fourth axis would rebuild the enumerated table the rule rejects.

### w0.Q7
Add a new work-type. Do not stretch a neighbour to cover the work, "because a stretched work-type carries two evidence contracts under one name" (W0 Territory, "The set grows when the territory finds a gap"). Until the new type exists, "the work is recorded against the gap, as a row in the project's backlog" (AR5). The new work-type is authored "from a peer exemplar (PC1), against the entry schema below, landing through the gate". Because adding one "changes how work is generated, so it is ratified by the director". The work must also qualify: it must "compile to a closeable claimable node". If it has no trigger and nothing to close, it is recorded as a routing note instead, as incident recovery is. W0 Faults names the cost of doing nothing: "The uncovered work ... is hand-routed every time".

### w0.Q8
W0 Faults lists five population faults that are "visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type". (1) Two work-types for one act, so the same node appears under two names. (2) The restated constraint: an entry copies the canonical set instead of satisfying it, and the copy drifts. (3) The doubly-declared pairing: a pairing stated on both the work-type and the domain, free to disagree (only the work-type's list governs). (4) The uncovered work: a recurring kind of work that no work-type covers, so it is hand-routed every time. (5) The uncited procedure: a procedure that conducts work but that no work-type cites, so a coordinating system cannot attach it. D0 Faults records that fault (3) once really happened: the two sides disagreed in 23 places. W0's "Gaps recorded" already lists live instances of (4) and (5): corpus-changing work has no build/fix/retire/land type, and procedures are "cited by only some work-types".

### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 Territory, "Gaps recorded", contradicts itself in two consecutive sentences. First: "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." Then: "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." D0's tie-break says the domain is where the diff lands, which seems to settle it. Both sentences remain, so it is unclear whether the governance/coordination-machinery gap is open.
- W22 contradicts itself and W0 constraint 2. Its frontmatter has `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`, but its `compositionHooks` and body say it seeds as "plain kind:review, not verifier-attestation per README constraint 2". Constraint 2 and R3 say verifier-held gates "never" use verifier-attestation.
- W22's scope is stated three ways: hydrate-when says "a single item", the title says "per-item", and the Definition says "a design or a set of items".
- `methods/README.md` line 105 says M7 and M8 "are cited by none". However, W22 and W14 both carry `methods: [M7]`. So M7 is cited, and the methods charter is stale.
- W0's entry schema lists `## Origin` as a body section, but neither W22 nor W23 has one.
- W0 cites "M7 guardrail #2", R4 cites "M7 guardrail #8", and W23 cites "the M7 guardrail". M7 contains no numbered guardrails, so these references do not resolve.
- `skills/workgraph-verification-gates/SKILL.md` line 182 ("unless exact authority explicitly accepts a weaker path") and its advisory-review "authority downgrade" are in tension with W0 constraint 9: "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation".
- W23 cites constraint 8, which is about author != approver for code-owner/independence gates, as "the director-ratification path is the sanctioned authority". That fits loosely at best.
- The links from work to M7 and AR4 run one way only. AR4's `related` list does not include W23, and M7 does not name W22.
