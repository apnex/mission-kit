### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is the set for this moment: "how an agent leads a human of limited context to understand and decide". It routes to its two members: S15 for "the form of any message to a human" and M10 for "leading a human to understanding, then a decision".
S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks for "Point first: the first line is the conclusion", so the slip goes in line one. It also asks for one step and one question at the end, explained names or none, and Continue/Explore/Return picks. If the slip needs a response, it asks for the decision frame: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate".
M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks you to know the finish line, give one confirmed step per message, and record any decision (in AR4, captured through W23).
A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) forbids going "silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed", and asks that you never narrow the options or pre-empt the choice. S0 (style/README.md) has a matching trigger, "a message a human will read", and points to S15.

### explain-routing.Q2

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame governs the form: what is being decided, then "why now" (the deadline), then each option with its cost, including doing nothing. Then give a labelled, separate recommendation and ask for a pick. S15 also says "the options are the picks: each option is a Continue pick naming it."
M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message and checks that the option set is complete with "none ... hidden". The director should be able to "answer with a pick, not a paragraph". Step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning, and to say where in one line.
A13 says you may recommend but never rework, narrow or pre-empt the options. A revealed-preference lean may appear only as a "labeled tie-break input".
AR4 and W23 cover recording and ratifying the result, and R4 says ratification "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative". ST1 and S0 route here.
K5 (questions asked several at once) and AR3 (many options shown together) are related only, each with a tension ST1 has not resolved. NOT SETTLED: S15's "two or three picks" does not fit three options plus doing nothing, each as a Continue pick.
