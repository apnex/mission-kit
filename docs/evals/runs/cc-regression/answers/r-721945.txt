### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame applies: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick". When a decision is asked for, "each option is a Continue pick naming it", so three options means three Continue picks, with Explore and Return kept. M10 step 4 asks you to check that the option set is complete, with "none ... hidden", and step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning and say where in one line. A13 asks that you never narrow or rework the option set or pre-empt the decision, and that any preference lean is labelled. AR4 and W23 are where the decision is recorded and ratified (ST1 lists them as related). The deadline maps to S15's "why now". NOT SETTLED: S15 says "End with two or three picks" and also says each option is a Continue pick, so three options plus Explore/Return gives up to five picks, and no entry settles which rule wins.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main guide: "one decision at a time", "Ask for one decision per message", and step 3: "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first, and confirm it has landed". So ask the first decision, record it, then frame the second. S15 sets each message's form: point first, one question at the end, the decision frame, "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line" (say there are two decisions), and Return picks to name what is parked. A13 forbids narrowing the second decision's options on the first one's behalf. ST1 gathers M10 and S15, and notes AR3 "offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time" as an unresolved tension. AR4 and W23 hold where each decision is recorded and ratified; AR4's `amends`/`supersedes` fields let the second ruling refer to the first. NOT SETTLED: M10 step 3 talks about a decision depending on understanding, not on another decision; that it also covers decision-on-decision is GUESS.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", but also that "each option is a Continue pick naming it". With three options (Q5), the two rules conflict, and neither ST1 nor M10 settles which one wins.
- S5's trigger ("name a version, date or other point-in-time identifier in prose") matches any message that names a slip date, but its body is scoped to user-facing documents. The trigger is broader than the rule.
- ST1 records its own gaps: bad news and teaching were each tested in only one pair, and no move is isolated. So Q1 and Q4 rest on general guidance, not on tested specific guidance.
- RU1's trace ("every summary of a triage, sweep or review leads with the delivered outcome") is close to a progress update. ST1 handles this by keeping RU1 related, not a member, but a cold agent reading RU1 alone may apply it.
- M10's Rationale says the director "proceeded from the decide limit without confirming it in words". So the decide limit (which Q5 and Q6 rely on) is less confirmed than the understand limit.
- The evidence that S15 and M10 cite (docs/evals/human/...) is outside this corpus, so I could not check it.
- INDEX.md tells the reader to fetch from GitHub main when there is no local checkout. That conflicts with the instruction for this task to read only this corpus. I followed the task instruction.
