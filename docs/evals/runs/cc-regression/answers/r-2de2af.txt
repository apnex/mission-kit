### w0.Q3
`domainFreedom` is a field on the work-type (W0 entry schema: `domainFreedom: free | pinned`), not on the domain (D0: "`domainFreedom` therefore lives on the **work-type**").
Free: object-level build, ship or assurance work "*acts on* a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice", and the generator can vary it (D0, Bimodal freedom; D1, D2, D3 and D7 are reached this way).
Pinned: meta or substrate work "whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it", and such work lands on D4, D5 or D6 (D0).
W0 constraint 3 says the same: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and the schema says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".
Examples: W22 and W23 are both `domainFreedom: pinned` to `authority-governance`.
W0 says the "aggregate > sum" claim is "honestly `pinned` for the meta third", in other words orthogonality is partial (A3).

### w0.Q4

W0 constraint 2: if the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent, "**fail the seed**, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."
Constraint 9: in a thin roster, degradation is allowed "ONLY" to "**defer-until-independent-seat** or **director-ratification**", and "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation"; it also may never route to an architect who is, or could be, the executor.

### w0.Q5
W0, "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in":
1. The systems the change alters. These give the traits, and so the axioms in force (E5, T0). The organisation doing the work is also a system, and its axioms govern how the work is done and recorded.
2. The work-type. It gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence.
3. The domain. This is the surface the work's evidence lands on (D0).
4. The role. This is who performs the work; what it may attest is set by role and work-type together (R0).
5. The method and artifact, which are the ones the work-type cites in its `methods` and `produces` fields.
W0 says everything outside the role x work-type x domain triple attaches "by citation or by system, never by widening the triple".

### w0.Q8
W0's Faults section lists these population faults, "visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type":
- Two work-types for one act: the same node can be minted under two names.
- The restated constraint: an entry copies the canonical constraint set, and the copy drifts.
- The doubly-declared pairing: a pairing is stated on both the work-type and the domain, and the two can disagree (only the work-type's list governs).
- The uncovered work: a recurring kind of work that no work-type covers, so it is hand-routed every time.
- The uncited procedure: a procedure that conducts work but that no work-type cites, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
W0 also says a stretched work-type (two evidence contracts under one name) is the wrong way to fill a gap.
