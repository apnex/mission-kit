Question:
What does it mean for a work-type to be 'pinned' rather than 'free' on the domain axis?

Key:
Pinned: the work-type names its own surface, so its domain is a constant of the type and the generator does not vary it; free: the domain is a discriminating choice among the type's eligible domains.

Rubric:
2 for both. 1 for one. 0 otherwise.

--- answer a-ecada599 ---
`domainFreedom` is a property of the work-type, not of the domain (D0 "Bimodal freedom"; W0 constraint 3).
**free**: object-level build, ship and assurance work "*acts on* a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice", and the generator can vary it across the work-type's `domainEligibility` (object-level surfaces D1, D2, D3, D7).
**pinned**: meta or substrate work "whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it", and it lands on D4, D5 or D6 (D0).
W0 constraint 3: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and the schema says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".

W0 explains that pinning is why the "aggregate > sum" claim holds fully only for the free third and "is honestly `pinned` for the meta third".

Key:
No. Same-agent review is never a valid degradation: the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification. The agent still self-checks by unbiased means, and that self-check never seals the gate.

Rubric:
2 for no, with wait-or-ratify ('fail the seed and route to director ratification' counts). 1 for no without the alternative. 0 for yes, or for a downgrade to plain review counting as the check.

Question:
In what order does an agent assemble what governs one unit of work, and where does each part come from?

Key:
The systems the change alters give the traits, and so the axioms in force (the organisation's own axioms, governing how the work is done and recorded, may be added); the work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its evidence authority says who may satisfy the evidence; the domain is the surface the work's evidence lands on; the role is who performs it, with what it may attest set by role and work-type together; the method and artifact are those the work-type cites.

2 for all five parts with their sources, in a workable order. 1 for three or four. 0 otherwise.

--- answer a-703d8564 ---

(c) The axioms that bind are those of each system the change alters, read from that system's traits through `applies-to`, plus the `any-system` floor (A0 "What brings an axiom into force"; T0 "How traits compose"; E5). The organisation's own axioms govern how the work is done, as a separate question (E5 lines 27-31).
(d) Situated moves (methods, rules, practices, style, skills) apply when their INDEX.md "Hydrate when" condition matches (INDEX.md; M0 places procedures at the six moments of a unit of work).
GUESS: one plausible order is to compose the triple, then derive authority and independence, then run the seed-time preflight, then find the binding axioms from traits, then hydrate the situated entries whose triggers match. The corpus does not state this order.
