The nine are: role-eligibility permissive/authority strict; structural, roster-aware independence; domain eligibility gates the pairing; targetRef/references required for relatedness gates; falsifier required; repair path for completion-gated seeds; generation mode gates idle-pooling; author != approver; bounded degradation.

### w0.Q4
No. W0 constraint 2: if the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent, "**fail the seed**, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."
Constraint 9: in a thin roster the check may degrade "to **defer-until-independent-seat** or **director-ratification** ONLY ... Same-agent review is never a valid degradation."
Marking it as weaker does not change this either. R0 says self-checks (even by a fresh instance of the same agent) "are mandatory; they do not seal a gate that requires independence", and "only its standing as assurance changes."
The agent must still check its own work, using means its own reasoning cannot bias (R0 "Checking one's own work is always required").
The director ratifying does not make the director a verifier (R0).
The closest "weaker" mechanism is `executor-evidence-provisional` (W0 constraint 7), but that closure "does not count as assurance until an independent gate consumes it", so it does not replace the attestation.

### w0.Q5
W0 "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in":
1. The systems the change alters give the traits, and so the axioms in force (E5, T0). The organisation doing the work is also a system, and its axioms govern how the work is done and recorded. E5 notes that the organisation is not one of the systems the change alters.
2. The work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence.
3. The domain is the surface the evidence lands on (D0; tie-break: "the evidence-target wins").
4. The role is who performs it. What it may attest is set by role and work-type together (R0).
5. The method and artifact are the ones the work-type cites in `methods` and `produces`.
W0: "Everything else attaches by citation or by system, never by widening the triple."

### w0.Q8
W0 "Faults" (population faults, "visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type"):
- Two work-types for one act: the same node minted under two names.
- The restated constraint: a work-type copies the canonical set and the copy drifts.
- The doubly-declared pairing: a pairing stated on both the work-type and the domain. Only the work-type's list governs, and the domain view is generated.
- The uncovered work: a recurring kind of work with no work-type, so it is hand-routed every time.
- The uncited procedure: a method that conducts work but no work-type cites it, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
W0 Territory adds stretched work-types carrying two evidence contracts under one name. E3 says a charter states "what a healthy population looks like as against a merely valid one."

### OBSERVATIONS
- W22 frontmatter and body declare `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`. W0 constraint 2 and R3 say verifier-held gates use plain `kind:review`, "never verifier-attestation". W22 resolves this by seeding as kind:review, but it is ambiguous whether the evidenceAuthority field itself should be verifier-attestation.
- W22's title and INDEX trigger say "per-item" / "a single item". Its definition says "a design or a set of items ... one item at a time". This makes it unclear whether a set-level check is the same work-type.
- W0 cites "M7 guardrail #2", and W23 cites "the M7 guardrail" that director-ratification cannot be satisfied by architect narrative. M7, as read, has no numbered guardrails and no such statement; that rule appears in R0.
- The W0 entry schema requires a `## Origin` body section, but W22 and W23 have none.
- The methods/produces links are stated only on the work-type side. M7 and AR4 do not point back. W0 admits that only some work-types cite methods and fewer cite artifacts.
- The INDEX tells readers to resolve links against a GitHub URL. This eval restricted reading to the local corpus, so I did not fetch anything remote.
- W0 assembly step 1 groups "the organisation is a system too" under "systems the change alters". E5 clarifies that the organisation's axioms are not one of the systems the change alters. The wording could be misread.
