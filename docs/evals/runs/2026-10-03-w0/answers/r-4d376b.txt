### w0.Q1
work-types/README.md (W0) lines 51-55: the nine-item "canonical constraint set" is the composition rule, and "The `closeabilityPreflight` of a work-type is exactly the seed-time projection of this set - a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them."
A generated node that cannot pass the preflight at seed "is not admitted" (line 55), which W0 says is what makes the catalog safe for autonomous generation (A7, A8).
The nine constraints cover: permissive eligibility but strict authority, structural roster-aware independence, domain eligibility, required targetRef/references, a required falsifier, a repair path for completion-gated seeds, idle-pool gating, author != approver, and bounded degradation (lines 57-96).
May an entry restate them? No. W0 line 13 says "the constraint set is authored here once and never forked per entry", and line 121 says the `## Evidence & closeability` section should "reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it".
README.md (root) "Faults" names restating as a fault, "The forked constraint": "A rule restated in a second place instead of referenced. Both copies read as authoritative and they drift silently."
An entry may still name which constraint carries the load for it. For example, W8 and W23 say "Load-bearing here: constraint 2" or "constraint 8" and do not copy the set.

### w0.Q3
W0 line 25-27 and D0 "Bimodal freedom" (domains/README.md lines 395-409) give the definitions.
**Free**: object-level build/ship/assurance work "*acts on* a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice". D1, D2, D3 and D7 are reached this way.
**Pinned**: meta/substrate work "whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it". Pinned work lands on D4, D5 or D6.
W0 constraint 3 (line 67) says "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain". The entry schema (line 113) says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".
`domainFreedom` is declared on the work-type, not on the domain (D0 line 409), and domain-side lists are "not consulted" (D0 lines 405-406, 429).
Example: W22 is `domainFreedom: pinned` with `domainEligibility: [authority-governance]`, and W8 is `free` across all seven domains.
W0 line 32 admits the cost: the "aggregate > sum" claim is "honestly `pinned` for the meta third".

### w0.Q4
No. Its own review cannot stand in, and it cannot stand in marked as weaker either.
W0 constraint 9 (line 92-96): "In a thin roster, an own-seat independence check may degrade to **defer-until-independent-seat** or **director-ratification** ONLY ... Same-agent review is never a valid degradation."
Constraint 2 (line 59-64) says the attester must be an identity "that **cannot be the executor given the LIVE roster**". If the roster puts attester and executor in one agent, the seed fails or is downgraded.
roles/README.md (R0) lines 288-291: "the independence a gate requires never falls to the same agent. It waits for a second agent, or the gate is met by the director's ratification instead"; R3 frontmatter says the verifier "may never attest own executed work".
The agent must still check its own work. R0 lines 279-286 call that check "mandatory", say a fresh instance of the same model "is still a self-check", and say such checks "do not seal a gate that requires independence".
So the options are to wait for a second agent or to get director ratification (R0 also names this "concentrated position" cost). The agent records its self-check as its own evidence, not as assurance.

### w0.Q5
NOT SETTLED. No entry I read gives one ordered procedure for assembling everything that governs a unit of work.
The parts are stated in separate places. (a) The unit is generated as `role x work-type x domain -> WorkItem template + evidence-authority + independence constraints + QoS overlays` (W0 lines 36-44; root README "Mechanics"). Roles come from roles/ (R0), domains from domains/ (D0), and the composition rule and closeability preflight from work-types/README.md (W0).
(b) Evidence authority comes from `role x work-type`, never from the role alone (R0 "Purity is on ESSENCE only"). Whether a domain pairing is allowed and whether it is free or pinned comes from the work-type (D0 "How domains compose"; W0 constraint 3).
(c) The axioms that bind are those of each system the change alters, read from that system's traits through `applies-to`, plus the `any-system` floor (A0 "What brings an axiom into force"; T0 "How traits compose"; E5). The organisation's own axioms govern how the work is done, as a separate question (E5 lines 27-31).
(d) Situated moves (methods, rules, practices, style, skills) apply when their INDEX.md "Hydrate when" condition matches (INDEX.md; M0 places procedures at the six moments of a unit of work).
GUESS: one plausible order is to compose the triple, then derive authority and independence, then run the seed-time preflight, then find the binding axioms from traits, then hydrate the situated entries whose triggers match. The corpus does not state this order.

### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 (work-types/README.md) lacks the `Territory` and `Faults` headings that E4 requires of every charter. ST1 and M10 refer to a pending "W0 conversion", so the gap appears known but is not marked in W0.
- W0 line 121 requires a `## Origin` body section, but W22, W23, W8 and W2 (the ones I read) have none.
- W0 line 13 cites "M7 guardrail #2", W23 cites "the M7 guardrail" (director-ratification cannot be met by architect narrative), and R4 line 39 cites "M7 guardrail #8". M7 as written has no numbered guardrails and no such statement. These look like the W0 constraint numbers (2 and 8) attributed to M7.
- Constraint 2 says "Verifier-*held* gates use plain `kind:review`, never verifier-attestation", but W22 and W8 declare `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation` and then say they seed as plain `kind:review`. The frontmatter and the constraint read as contradictory.
- Constraint 2 allows "fail the seed or downgrade to `kind:review`" when the roster collapses attester and executor, while constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". A reader could take the downgrade as allowing a weaker self-review. The text does not reconcile the two.
- INDEX.md's trigger for W22 says "checking a single item", but W22's body says "a design or a set of items ... one item at a time".
- W14 says "M7 axiom-alignment-gate", which mixes up the method M7 with the work-type W22, and W22 does not cite M7.
- W0 line 28 says "K~=26" and line 22 "M=4"/"N=7". These counts match the current population, but they are point-in-time figures of the kind the root README admission rules discourage.
