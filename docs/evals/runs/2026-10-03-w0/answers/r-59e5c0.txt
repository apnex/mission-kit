### w0.Q1
The closeability preflight is "exactly the seed-time projection" of the nine-constraint canonical set authored in work-types/README.md (W0, "The canonical constraint set (= the closeability preflight)"); "A generated node that cannot pass this preflight at seed is not admitted".
The nine constraints are: role eligibility permissive but authority strict, structural roster-aware independence, domain eligibility gating, required targetRef/references, a required falsifier, a repair path for completion-gated seeds, generation-mode gating of the idle pool, author != approver, and bounded degradation.
No, an entry may not restate them: "a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them", and "the constraint set is authored here once and never forked per entry (M7 guardrail #2)" (W0 Purpose).
The entry schema repeats this for the body: `## Evidence & closeability` must "reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it".
W0 Faults names the violation "The restated constraint ... the copy drifts from the one source".
In practice entries cite the constraints that matter most to them by number without copying the set (e.g. W8 cites constraint 2, W23 cites constraint 8).

### w0.Q3
`domainFreedom` is a property of the work-type, not of the domain (D0 "Bimodal freedom"; W0 constraint 3).
**free**: object-level build, ship and assurance work "*acts on* a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice", and the generator can vary it across the work-type's `domainEligibility` (object-level surfaces D1, D2, D3, D7).
**pinned**: meta or substrate work "whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it", and it lands on D4, D5 or D6 (D0).
W0 constraint 3: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and the schema says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".
Examples: W22 and W23 are both `domainFreedom: pinned` to `[authority-governance]`, while W8 is `free` across all seven domains.
W0 explains that pinning is why the "aggregate > sum" claim holds fully only for the free third and "is honestly `pinned` for the meta third".

### w0.Q5
W0 "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in" gives five steps:
1. The systems the change alters give the traits, and through them the axioms in force. The organisation doing the work is a system too, and its axioms govern how the work is done and recorded (sources: E5, T0).
2. The work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence (the W* frontmatter).
3. The domain is the surface the work's evidence lands on (D0).
4. The role is who performs it, and what it may attest "is set by role and work-type together" (R0).
5. The method and artifact are the ones the work-type cites in its `methods` and `produces` fields.
W0 says these attach "by citation or by system, never by widening the triple", and the result must pass the closeability preflight at seed.

### w0.Q7
Per W0 Territory: "A recurring kind of work that compiles to a closeable claimable node and fits no work-type is closed by adding one, not by stretching a neighbour to cover it, because a stretched work-type carries two evidence contracts under one name".

### w0.Q8
W0 "Faults" lists the population faults, "visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type":
- two work-types for one act, so the same node appears under two names;
- a work-type that restates the canonical constraint set, so the copy drifts;
- a doubly-declared pairing on both the work-type and the domain (only the work-type's list governs);
- uncovered recurring work, hand-routed every time;
- a procedure that conducts work but is cited by no work-type, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
Two related signs from W0 Territory: a neighbour stretched to cover new work (two evidence contracts under one name), and work-types that do not sit in exactly one group row.
E3 says a charter should state "what a healthy population looks like as against a merely valid one". W0 gives this only as the fault list above, not as a positive statement of health.
