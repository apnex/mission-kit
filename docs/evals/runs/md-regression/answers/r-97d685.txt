M10 is central: "Decide: one decision at a time", step 4 "Ask for one decision per message", and step 3 "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first, and confirm it has landed".
So ask the first decision alone, record it (step 5), then frame the second in light of the first; M10 step 1 says to know the finish line and state how many steps remain (here, two decisions).
S15: each message carries one question, in the decision frame, with options as Continue picks and a Return pick naming anything parked.

AR4/W23: each ruling is separately recorded and ratified; AR4's "amends"/"supersedes" fields express a later ruling's relation to an earlier one.
