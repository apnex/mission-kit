### composition.P1
Thirteen axioms bind it: A1, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13 and A14. Only A2 is left out.
Method: axioms/README.md (A0) says "An axiom is in force for a system if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies at least one of the axiom's applies-to tags", and traits/README.md (T0) says "a system's obligations are the union of every axiom any of its traits brings into force, plus every axiom on the floor".
I applied each trait's Test. The service is stateful (T1, "writes something it will later read back"), multi-agent (T3, "work crosses from one independent actor to another") and llm-in-the-loop (T5, "would the system decide or act differently if the model's output were different?"). It is not declarative (T2).
Union: the floor (any-system) gives A3, A4, A8, A9 and A14. T1 adds A1. T3 adds A5, A6, A7, A10 and A13. T5 adds A5, A11 and A12. I checked each axiom's applies-to frontmatter against the A0 applicability matrix, and they agree.
A2 is applies-to [declarative] only, so it does not bind.
NOT SETTLED: "long-running" does not tell me whether the service is autonomous (T4 asks whether a human would see a failure first). It makes no difference here, because T4 brings only A7, A10 and A13, which T3 already brings.
Separately, entities/E5-system.md says the organisation doing the work is a system of its own, and its axioms govern how the work is done and recorded.

### composition.P2
Only the floor binds the formatter: A3, A4, A8, A9 and A14 (all applies-to [any-system]). A0 says "a plain library takes any-system", and E5 says "A one-shot script with no traits is a system, bound by the axioms every system carries".
It has none of the traits. It is stateless (T1: "a pure function" is not stateful), it is "a script run once" and so not declarative (T2), it has one actor (T3), and it has no model (T5).
GUESS: it is not autonomous, because a person running a CLI sees its failures (T4 "Not autonomous: an interactive session").
On A1: no, A1 does not constrain this codebase. A1 is applies-to [stateful], and A0 says "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does". What a domain's citation decides is "what it demands on that surface" (A0, D0), and D0 adds that "the same domain can be worked on in a stateful system or a stateless one".
E5 also says that "a stateless product built by a team that keeps records it relies on has A1 binding the team's records, not the product". So A1 could still govern your work records, but not the formatter.
NOT SETTLED: whether the formatter is in D1 at all, because D1 covers "the codebase of the product the organisation ships" and the question does not say the formatter is shipped.
