### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is the set for "communicat[ing] with a human who has limited context", and its members are S15 and M10. S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks for "Point first: the first line is the conclusion" (here, that the release slips), one question asked at the end, unseen system names explained or left out, and two or three Continue/Explore/Return picks. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks you to know the finish line, give one step per message, and if a decision follows (e.g. how to respond to the slip), present it with each option's cost "including doing nothing", then record it (AR4/W23). A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) applies because you consume director attention: "the system never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed - under-surfacing is as much a fault as over-surfacing", and no option may be narrowed. S0 (style/README.md) routes "a message a human will read" to S15 as the only message-medium rule. NOT SETTLED: no entry is specific to bad news, and ST1 records "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair; no move is isolated". RU1 (honest yield) is named in ST1 only as "triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report", so it does not govern this message.

### explain-routing.Q2

### explain-routing.Q4
ST1 covers having a human "understand" something, through M10 and S15. A13 asks that director attention be used efficiently. S0 routes the form to S15. NOT SETTLED: nothing addresses preparing the director to retell the concept to others, and ST1 records that teaching "was tested in one pair". ### explain-routing.Q5
S15 sets the decision frame: "what is being decided; why now [the deadline]; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick", and "each option is a Continue pick naming it". M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message in that frame and requires that every real option with its cost and doing nothing be included, "none is hidden"; step 5 asks you to record the decision and reasoning and say where in one line. A13 forbids reworking "the option set", narrowing choices or pre-empting the decision; a recommendation is allowed but must not be pre-selected, and the act of deciding is non-delegable. W23 (work-types/W23-capture-decision-and-ratify.md) and AR4 (artifacts/AR4-decision-record.md) govern capturing and recording the ratified ruling, including refuted alternatives and a status. ST1 frames all this and names AR3 and K5 as related but in tension. NOT SETTLED: how to handle the director not answering by the deadline. GUESS: state in "why now" what happens if the deadline passes, as the cost of doing nothing.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main guide: "one decision at a time", "Ask for one decision per message", "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first", and "say how many steps remain". S15 asks for "One step, one question", showing "the step, and the finish line", and the decision frame for each decision. So ask the first decision, record it (M10 step 5; AR4/W23), then ask the second in a later message. A13 forbids narrowing or pre-empting either choice, which includes not presuming the first answer when framing the second. ST1 notes AR3's tension ("offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time"); AR3's "a move whose dependency is unmet is not legal yet" is consistent with sequencing. NOT SETTLED: whether the first message should preview how the second decision depends on the first. GUESS: name it in "Show where you are" (step 1 of 2) without asking it.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", but also that for a decision "each option is a Continue pick" and "Explore and Return keep their meaning"; with three options plus "doing nothing" (which M10/S15 require) that yields four or more picks, contradicting the two-or-three limit (Q5).
- ST1 body names W24, K5, AR3 and RU1 as related, but its frontmatter `related` list omits them.
- ST1 names K5's "three questions a round" as a tension against "one question at a time"; the corpus does not reconcile it.
- A13 says status-chatter never reaches the director, yet M10/S15 cover updates to a human who "has not followed the work"; when a progress update is warranted is not settled.
- A13 `applies-to: [multi-agent, autonomous]`, so whether it binds a given situation depends on traits; I assumed it does.
- S15 and M10 cite evidence at `docs/evals/human/...`, which does not exist in this corpus, so the evidence could not be checked.
- It is unclear whether document style rules (e.g. S6 "markdown prose that someone else will read", S11) also apply to messages; S0 lists only S15 under the message medium.
- INDEX.md says to fetch from GitHub when no checkout exists; I used only the local corpus as instructed.
