### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's domainEligibility governs. W0 constraint 3 ("a (work-type x domain) outside the type's domainEligibility is rejected") and W0 Faults "The doubly-declared pairing": "only the work-type's list governs, and the domain's view is generated"; D0 "How domains compose": "The work-type owns the pairing... Each domain entry shows the work-types that act on it, generated from the work-types' own lists, so the two cannot disagree"; each domain entry says "Generated from each work-type's domainEligibility, the list that governs."
(b) The axiom's applies-to governs. A0 "What brings an axiom into force": "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does"; T0 Purpose: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its applies-to field, and it binds a system that has at least one of them."
The corpus does not address the conflict case in words for traits; the ruling follows from A0's "only field". Unlike domains, the trait lists are not marked generated, so a disagreement is possible (see OBSERVATIONS); today all five trait lists match the axioms' applies-to.

### OBSERVATIONS
- Trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists are hand-written (no GENERATED markers), duplicating applies-to - the same "doubly-declared" shape D0/W0 name as a fault; tools/check-traits.mjs only checks the vocabulary, not these lists. Currently consistent.
- W8 body says that if the roster collapses attester and executor "the seed fails or downgrades to kind:review", which can be read as permitting a same-agent review; W0 constraints 2 and 9 say "a same-agent review never stands in". Conflict relevant to P5.
- W22 frontmatter has evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation while its compositionHooks say "plain kind:review, not verifier-attestation per README constraint 2".
- No entry explicitly names the work-type for applying a migration to live records; W7's definition/evidence (release artifact, deploy log) fit it loosely; D7 gives only an analogy.
- D1 is scoped to "the shipped product's codebase"; internal or unshipped code (e.g. P2's formatter) has no obvious domain, though D0 claims to cover "every surface".
- T3's "inside the system" plus E5's "organisation is a system" leaves open whether agents operating a service make the service multi-agent (P1).
- R3 engagement-mode lists work-types (W12, W13) without domain qualification, which a reader could misread as product-data eligibility.
