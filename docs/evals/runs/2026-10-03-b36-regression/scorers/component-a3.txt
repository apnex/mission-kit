A3 names the existing module as a fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns." So your change must not add to it: "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches", and "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by bolting it onto one." Give the feature its own unit owning "exactly one concern" ("Law of One"), and have it interact with other units "only through declared adapters and contracts". If your change touches the utils module, the Mandate asks you to remove the accretion you touch. Cleaning up utils is "encouraged" but framed as "a change of its own, answering to every consumer whose interface it alters (`A0`)". Do not make the new unit a stable, depended-upon surface until "a real consumer outside its origin needs it" ("Earned Exposure"). NOT SETTLED: how much of utils "touches" covers, and whether that removal belongs in the feature change or in a separate one. GUESS: put the feature in a new single-concern unit, and split out only the parts of utils your feature actually uses, as a separate change.
