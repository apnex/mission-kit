### component-a3.C5
A3 names the existing module as a fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns." So your change must not add the feature to `utils`, because "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches", and "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by bolting it onto one." Put the feature in its own unit that does "exactly one thing" (Law of One), and have it reach other units "only through declared adapters and contracts". Where your change touches `utils`, it should remove the accretion there, and A3 encourages this: "fix it, fracture it, re-cut its seams and recompose it ... as a change of its own, answering to every consumer whose interface it alters (`A0`)." Do not promote the new unit to "a stable, depended-upon surface" unless "a real consumer outside its origin needs it" (Earned Exposure). NOT SETTLED: how much of `utils` counts as "what it touches", and whether that cleanup must ship inside the feature change or as the separate change the text describes. GUESS: you split out only the parts of `utils` your feature actually uses or edits, in a separate change before or alongside the feature, and leave the rest alone.

### OBSERVATIONS
- "removes what it touches" has no stated scope (a call site, a function, or the whole module?), and the text sends the reader to `A0` for "How an axiom binds a change", which this file does not contain.
- Possible tension: the Mandate asks the change itself to remove what it touches, but Composable by Default says improving an existing unit should be "a change of its own". The file does not say whether the cleanup belongs in the feature change.
- Success signal 3 ("New capabilities arrive by composition, not by modifying existing units") reads as stricter than Composable by Default, which encourages modifying existing units within their own duty. The two can be reconciled (adding a capability vs. improving a unit), but the signal does not say so.
- The frontmatter `related` lists only `A8`, while the body refers to `A0` twice.
