M10 step 4: one decision per message, with all real options and none hidden ("checking that needs the real options, so it is this step's"); step 5: record the decision and say where in one line.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 rules directly: "one decision at a time", "Ask for one decision per message", and step 1 "Know the finish line ... say how many steps remain" - so say that there are two decisions and ask for the first only.
M10 step 3: "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first, and confirm it has landed"; its rationale cites A8, "each step lands before the next builds on it".
S15: each message gives one step and one question at the end in the decision frame, shows "the step, and the finish line", and ends with picks, which can include Return to a parked item.
A13: do not narrow the second decision's options in advance based on a guess of the first; the director decides each.
AR4/W23: record each ruling (AR4 has an `amends` field for a later ruling that modifies an earlier one); M10 step 5 asks you to say where in one line.
ST1 lists K5 (Round 2 framed by Round 1 picks, but three questions per round) and AR3 (a dependency graph of moves) as related entries in tension with one decision at a time.
