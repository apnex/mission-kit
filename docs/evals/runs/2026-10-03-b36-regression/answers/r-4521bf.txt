### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is where to start: it collects the guidance for "communicat[ing] with a human who has limited context", and its two members are S15 and M10. S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks that the message open with the point ("the first line is the conclusion"), that system-specific names be explained or left out, and that it end with one question and two or three picks (Continue/Explore/Return). If the slip needs a decision, S15 also asks for its "decision frame": what is decided, why now, each option with its cost including doing nothing, and a recommendation labelled separately. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks you to go one confirmed step at a time, to build understanding before asking for any decision, and to record the decision and its reasoning (in AR4, via W23). A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) asks that you "never go silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed" (so you must surface the slip) and that you send "decision over status". S0 (style/README.md) matches by trigger ("a message a human will read"), but it only routes on to S15. Caveat from ST1: "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair", so the support for bad news in particular is thin. NOT SETTLED: whether S5 ("name a version, date ...") covers the new release date in a message; GUESS: it does not, because its body is about user-facing documentation prose.

### explain-routing.Q2
ST1 routes to S15 and M10, and M10 says it applies "especially when they have not followed the work". S15 asks for: the conclusion first; "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line"; every unseen name, label or ID explained or dropped, with references in one line; and narrative only "where it carries understanding". M10 asks that you know the finish line, say how many steps remain, give one step per message, and keep depth "available on request and never pushed". A13 asks that "Status-chatter ... never reach the Director": prefer "digest over dump" and keep what you send decision-shaped and gate-aligned, but without going "silently dark". RU1 (rules/RU1-default-reject-honest-yield.md) asks that a summary lead with the delivered outcome, but only when the update reports a sweep, refactor or audit; ST1 notes RU1 "is triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report". NOT SETTLED: whether a plain progress update counts as the status-chatter A13 forbids, or as surfacing the director is owed.

### explain-routing.Q4
ST1 routes to M10 and S15, and its Purpose covers a human who must "understand what an agent tells them". M10's understand limit asks that the human see what you see, with nothing missing and nothing extra, and step 3 says to confirm the understanding "has landed" before you build on it. S15 asks you to explain any system-specific name the director has not seen ("General engineering terms may be assumed"), to give one step per message, and to keep references out of the body. A13 asks you to economise their attention while keeping it on judgement. ST1 warns that "teaching [was] tested in one pair" only. NOT SETTLED: no entry addresses preparing someone to retell a concept to others. GUESS: confirming understanding per M10 step 3, for example by having them restate it, is the nearest guidance. K28 (STE) matches only documentation for non-native readers, so it does not fit here.

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame is the main guidance: say what is being decided and why now (the deadline), give each of the three options with its cost, add "doing nothing" with its cost, put a recommendation "labelled and kept separate", then ask for a pick. M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message with none hidden (checking completeness is M10's job, not the message's), and step 5 asks you to record the decision and its reasoning and say where in one line. A13 says you "never rework[] the option set, narrow[] the choices, nor pre-empt[] the decision", and that any preference lean appears only as a labelled tie-break. R4 (roles/R4-director.md) and W23 (work-types/W23-capture-decision-and-ratify.md) both make the choice the director's to ratify: ratification "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative". AR4 (artifacts/AR4-decision-record.md) is where the ruling goes, with refuted alternatives, named costs and a status. ST1 also lists AR3 as related but in tension, because a board "offers many moves at once, against one decision at a time". NOT SETTLED: what to do if the deadline passes with no pick, and how S15's "two or three" Continue/Explore/Return picks fit four decision options.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 sets the order: "one decision at a time", one decision per message (step 4), and record each decision before moving on (step 5). Step 3 also says to establish understanding before a decision that depends on it. S15 asks for one question per message, asked at the end, and asks you to "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line" (GUESS: for example, "decision 1 of 2"). Each message uses S15's decision frame. A13 forbids narrowing the second decision's options on the basis of the director's likely answer to the first. AR4 makes each ruling its own record ("One ruling is one record"), with amends and supersedes kept as separate fields. W23 is the work unit that captures and ratifies each one. AR3 models dependent moves as a graph ("a move whose dependency is unmet is not legal yet"), but ST1 flags it as in tension with one decision at a time, and likewise K5, whose Round 2 depends on Round 1 but which asks three questions per round. NOT SETTLED: whether the first message should preview the second decision's options or only say that it exists.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S5's trigger ("name a version, date or other point-in-time identifier in prose") also fires for a message that names a new release date, but its body is about user-facing documentation. Either the trigger is too broad or its scope is unclear.
- S0's trigger names "a message a human will read", but its body is about documents. It adds nothing for messages beyond pointing to S15.
- A13 says "Status-chatter ... never reach the Director", while the same axiom forbids going "silently dark". The corpus does not say which side a progress update falls on.
- S15 says to end with "two or three picks" (Continue/Explore/Return), but its decision frame needs every option plus doing nothing (four for Q5). It is unclear whether those two pick sets are the same thing.
- ST1's frontmatter `related` leaves out W24, K5, AR3 and RU1, although its body discusses each of them.
- M10 and S15 cite evidence at docs/evals/human/..., which is not in this corpus, so I could not verify it.
- A13 has `applies-to: [multi-agent, autonomous]`. Whether it binds a single agent writing to the director depends on traits (T0), which I did not settle.
- R4 lists the same "A13" bullet twice under Axiom alignment.
- INDEX.md says to resolve links "against a local mission-kit checkout ... otherwise fetch from GitHub", which conflicts with an instruction to read only this corpus. I used only local files.
