### component-a3.C5
Do not add the feature to `utils`: A3 names this exact shape as a fault, "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns", and the Mandate says "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches."
Build the feature as its own unit and compose it in: "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by bolting it onto one", and under the "Law of One", "a module does exactly one thing".
If your change touches the part of `utils` you depend on, the Mandate's "removes what it touches" asks you to separate that concern out, not leave it entangled.
That cleanup is allowed and encouraged, but "as a change of its own, answering to every consumer whose interface it alters" ("fix it, fracture it, re-cut its seams and recompose it").
The new unit gets an internal boundary because it is one concern, but it should not become a stable shared surface yet: "it earns promotion to a stable, depended-upon surface only when a real consumer outside its origin needs it."
NOT SETTLED: how much of `utils` counts as "what it touches" (only code you edit, or code you call), and whether you must restructure all of `utils` or only the concerns your change touches.
GUESS: only the concerns your change touches, because the text speaks of a change "short of" the limit, not one that reaches it.

### OBSERVATIONS
- "removes what it touches" (Mandate) and "as a change of its own" (Composable by Default) do not say clearly whether the cleanup goes in the feature change or in a separate change; it is ambiguous whether "of its own" means a separate commit/change or only that the improvement must answer for itself.
- Success signal 3 says "New capabilities arrive by composition, not by modifying existing units", but Composable by Default encourages modifying existing units ("fix it, fracture it, re-cut its seams"). These can be read together (modifying to improve a unit is fine, modifying to add a capability is not), but the signal's wording alone contradicts the mechanic.
- "touches" is not defined.
- The file points to `A0` (README.md) for how an axiom binds a change; I did not open it, as instructed, so that binding rule is not checked here.
