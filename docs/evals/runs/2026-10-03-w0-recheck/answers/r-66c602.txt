### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is the set for "communicate with a human who has limited context", and it names two members: S15 for "the form of any message to a human" and M10 for "leading a human to understanding, then a decision".
S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks: "Point first: the first line is the conclusion" (here, that the release will slip), one step and one question at the end, show the step and finish line, explain unfamiliar names, and end with two or three Continue/Explore/Return picks.
M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks that the director end up "seeing what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", one confirmed step at a time; if a response is needed, one decision per message with each option's cost "including doing nothing", then record the decision (step 5).
A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) asks that you never go "silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed - under-surfacing is as much a fault as over-surfacing", and that you recommend but never narrow the options or pre-empt the decision.
S0 (style/README.md) routes on "a message a human will read" but only points to S15.
ST1 records that RU1 (honest yield) is related but "triggered by sweeps and audits, not by any report", so it does not apply unless the slip report is a sweep summary.
NOT SETTLED: no entry is specific to bad news; ST1 records "Bad news and teaching were each tested in one pair".

### explain-routing.Q5
S15's decision frame applies: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick"; the first line is the decision asked for; "each option is a Continue pick naming it"; and a request to act is "never hidden in an optional pick".
The deadline belongs in "why now".
M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message, with every real option and doing nothing included and "none is hidden", so the director "can answer with a pick, not a paragraph"; step 5 records the decision and says where in one line.
A13 asks you to recommend but never "rework the option set, narrow the choices, nor pre-empt the decision", and to show any preference lean only as a labelled tie-break.
AR4 (decision record) and W23 (capture-decision-and-ratify) govern where the ruling is recorded and ratified; ST1 lists them as related, and M10 points to them.
R4 adds that ratification is the director's alone and "cannot be satisfied by architect narrative".
ST1 notes a tension with AR3 (board), which "offers many moves at once".
