### composition.P5
No. W8 applies when "You are gating a build or change that you did not author", and its falsifier includes "self-attestation". W0 constraint 2 says that if "the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent -> fail the seed, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."

Instead, check your own work anyway, because R0 says this is "always required" and "mandatory". Use means your reasoning cannot bias: "a mechanism that fails when the work is wrong, a fresh reader holding only the work, a measurement in place of a re-reading".
W4 validate-locally is the typed form of this, and its evidence is `executor-evidence-provisional`, which "does not count as assurance until an independent gate consumes it".

A fresh instance of yourself is still a self-check and cannot seal the gate (R0).

### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. W0 Faults: "only the work-type's list governs, and the domain's view is generated". D0 "How domains compose": "The work-type owns the pairing... Each domain entry shows the work-types that act on it, generated from the work-types' own lists, so the two cannot disagree". Each domain entry's table says "Generated from each work-type's `domainEligibility`, the list that governs."
(b) The axiom's `applies-to` field governs. axioms/README.md: "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does". traits/README.md: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its `applies-to` field".
The corpus never states in so many words "if a trait's list disagrees, applies-to wins". That reading is inferred from "the only field that does".
Unlike (a), the trait lists are not generated (no GENERATED markers in T1-T5), and tools/check-traits.mjs checks only the applies-to vocabulary against the trait names, not each trait's axiom list. So a disagreement could arise without anything catching it.
Today they agree: I compared T1-T5 against every axiom's `applies-to`.
