### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks", but also says that when asking for a decision "each option is a Continue pick naming it, and Explore and Return keep their meaning"; with three options (Q5) that yields three Continue picks plus Explore/Return, up to five - the two statements conflict.
- S15 and M10 cite evidence at `docs/evals/human/runs/guidance-1-*` and `docs/evals/human/MATRIX.md`; no `docs/` directory exists in this corpus, so the evidence is unverifiable from here.
- ST1 records its own gaps: bad news and teaching each tested in one pair, no move isolated, and the evaluation procedure exists "only as a tool and suites ... not as an entry".
- K5 (three questions per round) and AR3 (many moves at once) are flagged by ST1 as in tension with one-question/one-decision, but are not reconciled, so a director-facing survey or board has opposite guidance.
- INDEX.md says to fetch from a remote URL when no local checkout exists; I used only this corpus, per instructions.
- AGENTS.md carries relevant claim-discipline rules (caveat vs headline) but has no ID and is not in the ledger, so a ledger-driven agent would not route to it for director messages.
