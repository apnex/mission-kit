### w0.Q1
The closeability preflight is "exactly the seed-time projection of" the nine-constraint canonical constraint set in work-types/README.md (W0, "The canonical constraint set (= the closeability preflight)").
Those constraints cover: role eligibility permissive but authority strict, roster-aware independence, domain eligibility, required targetRef/references, a required falsifier, a repair path for completion-gated seeds, generation-mode gating of idle-pooling, author != approver, and bounded degradation.
"A generated node that cannot pass this preflight at seed is not admitted" (W0).
No, an entry may not restate them: "a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them", and "the constraint set is authored here once and never forked per entry (M7 guardrail #2)" (W0 Purpose).
The body-section schema repeats this: `## Evidence & closeability` must "reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it" (W0 entry schema). W0's Faults list names "The restated constraint" because "the copy drifts from the one source".
Entries do cite individual constraints as load-bearing (for example W8 cites constraint 2 and W13 cites constraint 8), and that is citation, not restatement.

### w0.Q3
`domainFreedom` is a field on the work-type, not on the domain (D0 "Bimodal freedom": "`domainFreedom` therefore lives on the **work-type**, not the domain").
**free**: object-level build/ship/assurance work "*acts on* a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice". The generator varies the domain within `domainEligibility`. D1, D2, D3 and D7 are reached this way (D0).
**pinned**: "meta/substrate work whose *type names its own surface*: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it". Such work lands on D4, D5 or D6 (D0). W0 constraint 3: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and the schema says `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned".
Example: W22 and W23 are pinned to `authority-governance` (their frontmatter; D4 line 21). W8 is free across all seven domains.
D4/D5/D6 remain valid free targets too, for example as surfaces to audit (D0). W0 says the `aggregate > sum` claim holds fully only for the free third and is "honestly `pinned`" for the meta third.

### w0.Q4
No, and not even when marked as weaker.
W0 constraint 2: if the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent, "**fail the seed**, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."
Constraint 9: in a thin roster the only permitted degradations are "**defer-until-independent-seat** or **director-ratification** ONLY ... Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". It also may never route to the architect when the architect is, or is eligible as, the executor.
roles/README.md (R0) agrees: "When there is no second agent, the independence a gate requires never falls to the same agent", and "the director does not become a verifier".
The agent must still check its own work: R0 calls such checks "mandatory", and says even a fresh no-context instance of itself "is still a self-check" and "cannot provide independent assurance". These checks "do not seal a gate that requires independence".
The corpus provides only one way to have weaker evidence count: `executor-evidence-provisional` (constraint 7, W4), whose "closure does not count as assurance until an independent gate consumes it". It does not replace the attestation.
So the options are: wait for a second agent, or obtain director ratification.

### w0.Q5

### w0.Q8
W0 "Faults" lists population faults, which are "visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type":
- Two work-types for one act, so a generator mints the same node under two names.
- The restated constraint: an entry that copies the canonical constraint set, and the copy drifts.
- The doubly-declared pairing: domain pairing stated on both the work-type and the domain, free to disagree. Only the work-type's list governs, and the domain view is generated.
- The uncovered work: recurring work no work-type covers, so it is hand-routed every time.
- The uncited procedure: a method no work-type cites, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
W0 Territory implies more: a stretched work-type carrying two evidence contracts, and a work-type sitting in no group row or in more than one.
I measured that the current set already shows the uncited-procedure fault. Only M1, M7 and M10 are cited (W8, W10, W14, W22, W24). M2, M8 and M9 are cited by no work-type, which W0 partly acknowledges under "Gaps recorded".

### OBSERVATIONS
- W22 contradicts itself and W0 constraint 2. Its frontmatter says `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`, and its Evidence section says "`evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`, held by the verifier". But its compositionHooks and body say that, as a verifier-held gate, it seeds as "plain kind:review, not verifier-attestation". Constraint 2 says "Verifier-held gates use plain `kind:review`, never verifier-attestation". W8 (`verifier-attestation`, verifier-only) has a similar tension.
- W8 says "if the live roster collapses attester and executor, the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`". That can be read as permitting a same-agent review, which conflicts with W0 constraint 2 ("a same-agent review never stands in") and constraint 9.
- W22's title and hydrate-when say "per-item" or "a single item", while its Definition covers "a design or a set of items ... one item at a time". This can be reconciled, but it may misroute someone checking a set.
- W22 cites M7, but M7's Rule is scoped to "extensive planning or design exercise[s]", not per-item checks. M7 and AR4 do not link back to the work-types that cite them.
- W23 cites "constraint 8 (the director-ratification path is the sanctioned authority)". Constraint 8 is about author != approver for independence gates, and mentions director ratification only as the unblock path. The citation is loose.
- W0's entry schema requires an `## Origin` body section, but none of the 26 work-type entries has one (measured by grep). Also, `closeabilityPreflight` is named as if it were a field, but it does not appear in the frontmatter schema. It is a projection, not a field.
- W0 says "Every work-type sits in one row". I checked: all 26 (W1-W26) appear exactly once across the seven groups.
- INDEX.md tells readers to fetch from GitHub main when no local checkout exists, but this eval restricted reading to the local corpus copy. I followed the restriction.
