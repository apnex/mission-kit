### explain-routing.Q1
ST1 (sets/ST1-explain.md) is the routing set for "communicate with a human who has limited context"; its members are S15 and M10, and it says bad news "was tested in one pair" only (Gaps recorded). S15 (style/S15-message-to-a-human.md) asks: "Point first: the first line is the conclusion" (so the slip itself leads), explain any system-specific name, show the step and finish line, use narrative only where it carries understanding, and end with Continue/Explore/Return picks. M10 (methods/M10-guided-dialogue.md) asks that the director end up "seeing what you see on the point - nothing missing that matters, nothing extra", and if a decision follows (e.g. re-plan), use the decision frame with each option's cost "including doing nothing". A13 (axioms/A13-director-intent-amplification.md) asks for "decision over status" and sets a floor: "the system never goes silently dark on an action or decision the Director is owed" - so the slip must be surfaced. S0 (style/README.md) routes here ("a message a human will read") and points to S15. No entry is specific to delivering bad news: NOT SETTLED beyond the general rules. GUESS: S5's trigger ("name a version, date") would fire on a release date, but its "When to apply" scopes it to user-facing docs, not messages, so it likely does not govern this.

### explain-routing.Q5
S15 sets the decision frame: "what is being decided; why now; the options, each with its cost, including doing nothing; a recommendation, labelled and kept separate; then ask for a pick", with the decision in the first line; "the options are the picks: each option is a Continue pick naming it". M10 step 4 asks for one decision per message and that the options be complete, "none is hidden", and step 5 asks you to record the decision and reasoning and say where in one line. The deadline belongs in "why now"; no entry gives deadline-specific guidance beyond that (NOT SETTLED). A13 forbids narrowing: never "reworks the option set, narrows the choices, nor pre-empts the decision", and a lean may appear only as a labelled tie-break. ST1 routes here; W23 and AR4 cover capturing and recording the decision (AR4: refuted alternatives, status, authority; W23: ratification must come from the director, not architect narrative). R4 confirms the decide act is the director's alone. AR3 (board) is related with a tension: it presents many moves at once, against M10's one decision at a time.

### explain-routing.Q6
M10 is the main guide: "one decision at a time", "Ask for one decision per message", know the finish line and say how many steps remain, and "If a decision depends on understanding, establish the understanding first". S15 asks you to "Show where you are: the step, and the finish line", one question at the end, decision frame per message, and offers a Return pick to come back to something parked. A13 bars pre-empting the second decision or narrowing its options on the director's behalf. W23/AR4 cover recording each decision (AR4 has an `amends`/`supersedes` field pair and states each ruling must be "separately addressable"). AR3 is the corpus's only treatment of dependency between decisions ("a move whose dependency is unmet is not legal yet"; "Decisions required ... naming exactly what it blocks"), but ST1 flags its tension with one-decision-at-a-time. Sequencing two dependent decisions is NOT SETTLED explicitly. GUESS: ask the first, say a dependent second follows (step 1 of 2), and frame the second only after the first is recorded.

### OBSERVATIONS
- S15 says "End with two or three picks" yet also says each decision option is a Continue pick, alongside Explore and Return; three options plus doing nothing plus Explore/Return exceeds three picks. Inconsistent for Q5.
- A13 says status-chatter never reaches the director and "decision over status", while M10/S15 support progress updates to someone who has not followed the work; the boundary between an owed update and status-chatter is not drawn.
- ST1 itself records tensions with K5 (three questions per round) and AR3 (many moves at once) against one-decision-at-a-time, unresolved.
- S5's hydrate-when fires on any date in prose (e.g. a slipped release date), but its body scopes it to user-facing docs; a cold agent could misapply it to a message.
- ST1 records no entry for evaluating a message on its human, and bad news/teaching each rest on one test pair.
