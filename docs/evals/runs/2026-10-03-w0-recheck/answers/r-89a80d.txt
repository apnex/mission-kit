### w0.Q1
The closeability preflight is the seed-time projection of the nine-constraint canonical set in `work-types/README.md` (W0): "The `closeabilityPreflight` of a work-type is exactly the seed-time projection of this set - a work-type does not restate them, it *satisfies* them."
A generated node that cannot pass it at seed "is not admitted" (W0, "The canonical constraint set").
The constraints cover role-eligibility vs strict evidence authority, roster-aware independence, domain eligibility, required targetRef/references, a named falsifier, a repair path for completion-gated seeds, generation-mode gating of the idle pool, author != approver, and bounded degradation.
No, an entry may not restate them: W0 says "the constraint set is authored here once and never forked per entry (M7 guardrail #2)", and the body-section spec says `## Evidence & closeability` must "reference this file's constraint set - do not restate it".
Restating is also listed as a population fault, "The restated constraint ... the copy drifts from the one source" (W0, Faults).
Entries may cite a specific constraint as load-bearing (e.g. W8 cites constraint 2, W23 cites constraint 8) while saying "do not restate it here".

### w0.Q3
`domainFreedom` is a field on the work-type (W0 entry schema; D0 "`domainFreedom` therefore lives on the work-type, not the domain").
Free: object-level build/ship/assurance work "acts on a surface, so the domain is a free, discriminating choice" - e.g. W8 lists all seven domains (`domains/README.md`, "Bimodal freedom").
Pinned: meta/substrate work "whose type names its own surface: the domain is a constant function of the work-type (N=1), so the generator does not vary it", and pinned work lands on D4, D5 or D6 (D0).
Constraint 3 in W0: "`domainFreedom: pinned` means the generator does not vary the domain", and `domainEligibility` is "a single value when pinned" (W0 schema) - e.g. W22 and W23 are pinned to `authority-governance`.
W0 says the `aggregate > sum` claim holds fully for the free third and "is honestly `pinned` for the meta third - stated, not over-claimed (A3)".

### w0.Q4
No. W0 constraint 2: if the live roster collapses attester and executor to one agent, "fail the seed, and the gate waits for an independent agent or is met by director ratification (constraint 9); a same-agent review never stands in."
Constraint 9: in a thin roster the check "may degrade to defer-until-independent-seat or director-ratification ONLY ... Same-agent review is never a valid degradation"; it also may not route to the architect if the architect is (or could be) the executor.
`roles/README.md` agrees: the agent must still check its own work (mandatory, using means its own reasoning cannot bias, such as a mechanism, a fresh reader or a measurement), but "they do not seal a gate that requires independence"; even a fresh instance of the same agent "is still a self-check".
So "marked as weaker" is not an option for a verifier-attestation gate. The text says the same of `executor-evidence-provisional`: under constraint 7 its closure "does not count as assurance until an independent gate consumes it". It does not replace the independent attestation.
Note that W8's "Evidence & closeability" text ("the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`") can be read as allowing a downgrade; see OBSERVATIONS. W0 is canonical and forbids it.

### w0.Q5
W0, "The order a cold agent assembles one unit of work in":
1. The systems the change alters give the traits, and so the axioms in force (E5, T0); the organisation doing the work is a system too, and its axioms govern how work is done and recorded.
2. The work-type gives the eligible roles and domains, and its `evidenceAuthority` says who may satisfy the evidence.
3. The domain is the surface the work's evidence lands on (D0).
4. The role is who performs it; what it may attest is set by role and work-type together (R0).
5. The method and artifact are the ones the work-type cites (its `methods` and `produces` fields).
The underlying rule is `role x work-type x domain -> WorkItem template`, plus constraints. Method and system attach "by citation or by system, never by widening the triple" (W0, composition rule).

### w0.Q7
W0, Territory: it is "closed by adding one, not by stretching a neighbour to cover it, because a stretched work-type carries two evidence contracts under one name; until it exists, the work is recorded against the gap."
The bar is that it "compiles to a closeable claimable node" (also the taxonomy-grade test in the entry schema section). If it cannot compile (no trigger, nothing to close), it becomes a routing note instead.
A new work-type is authored from a peer exemplar (PC1), against the W0 entry schema, and lands through the gate. "Adding one changes how work is generated, so it is ratified by the director."
"The uncovered work" (hand-routed every time) is listed as a population fault.
NOT SETTLED: where exactly the gap record lives. The only example is W0's own "Gaps recorded" paragraph ("Extending this corpus ... has no work-type"). GUESS: the agent adds a line there, or files a backlog item (AR5).

### w0.Q8
W0, Faults ("visible across the set, invisible to any one work-type"):
- Two work-types for one act: the same node can be minted under two names.
- The restated constraint: an entry copies the canonical set instead of satisfying it, and the copy drifts.
- The doubly-declared pairing: a work-type/domain pairing stated on both sides, which can disagree. Only the work-type's list governs, and the domain view is generated. D0 records that this once disagreed in 23 places.
- The uncovered work: a recurring kind of work with no work-type, so it is hand-routed every time.
- The uncited procedure: a procedure no work-type cites, so a coordinating system cannot attach it.
Related signs from Territory: a stretched work-type carrying two evidence contracts, and a work-type that fits no group row ("Every work-type sits in one row").

### OBSERVATIONS
- W8 "Evidence & closeability" says "if the live roster collapses attester and executor, the seed fails or downgrades to `kind:review`". W0 constraint 2 and 9 say the seed fails and "a same-agent review never stands in". The "downgrades" wording can be read as allowing what W0 forbids.
- W0 constraint 2 says "Verifier-*held* gates use plain `kind:review`, never verifier-attestation". Yet W8 and W22 both declare `evidenceAuthority: verifier-attestation`, and W22's body says it is "held by the verifier" and seeds as plain `kind:review`. The line between `evidenceAuthority` and seeded node kind is not explained, and it reads as self-contradictory.
- W22's hydrate-when and title say "single item" / "per-item", but its Definition covers "a design or a set of items". M7 is scoped to "extensive planning or design exercise", not item sets. The fit between W22 and M7 is loose.
- W0 calls for the body section `## Origin`, but W8, W22 and W23 have none.
- `methods`/`produces` links go one way only (M7 does not cite W22, AR4 does not cite W23). W0 admits citations are partial ("cited by only some work-types, and artifacts by fewer").
- W0's "M7 guardrail #2" (never fork the constraint set) does not appear in M7 as a numbered guardrail. M7 has no numbered guardrails, so the citation does not resolve.
- W23 cites "the M7 guardrail" that director-ratification cannot be satisfied by architect narrative. M7 does not say this; R0 does.
