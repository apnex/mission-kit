### component-a3.C5
First, do not add the feature to the 'utils' module: A3 names exactly this as a fault, "God-Object Accretion - 'utils', 'helpers', 'managers' accumulate unrelated concerns", and says "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches" (where "them" is "god objects, spaghetti coupling and dual-purpose modules").
Give the feature its own unit owning one concern ("Law of One - a module does exactly one thing; 'and'/'also' in its description is a violation"), and assemble it by composition ("a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by modifying them").
It should interact with other units "only through declared adapters and contracts" (Air-Gap Principle), so it stays understandable "in isolation, from its contract and inputs alone".
Do not expose it as a stable, depended-upon surface yet: "it earns promotion to a stable, depended-upon surface only when a real consumer outside its origin needs it"; otherwise it is a "Speculative Surface".
If your change does touch the 'utils' module, "removes what it touches" asks you to remove the accretion in the part you touch.
NOT SETTLED: how much "what it touches" covers (only the code you edit, or the whole 'utils' module), and the text does not require a full refactor of 'utils' as part of a small feature.
GUESS: it means only the concerns your change actually edits or depends on in 'utils', which you would split into their own single-concern units, leaving the rest of 'utils' alone.
