### composition.P5
No. W8's trigger is "a build or change that you did not author", its falsifier includes "self-attestation", and R3 "may never attest own executed work".
R0 says the identity constraint holds even when one agent holds every role: "The same identity may not be both the executor and the independent verifier of one piece of work."
Instead, you still check your own work, which is mandatory. Use means your reasoning cannot bias: "a mechanism that fails when the work is wrong, a fresh reader holding only the work, a measurement in place of a re-reading" (R0). ### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 constraint 2 says that when the roster collapses attester and executor, the seed must "fail the seed or downgrade to kind:review". Constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". For a lone agent, a downgrade to kind:review looks like a same-agent review unless someone else holds the review. This is ambiguous.
- No tie-break rule names a trait list disagreeing with applies-to. tools/check-traits.mjs (I read its header) checks only the applies-to enum against trait names, not each trait's "Axioms it brings into force" list. That list is a second copy that could drift without any check catching it.
- W7's title and definition talk about "the estate/channel" and "release artifact". D0 contrasts the estate (distribution) with records (product-data). So placing a migration apply in W7 rests on its domainEligibility and D7's analogy, not on W7's own definition.
- D1's (and D0's) A1 alignment is about the taxonomy's own node evidence, not about the product system's traits. A reader can easily take it as A1 binding every codebase.
- R3's engagement-mode list (which includes meta-validate-dogfood and code-owner-approve) is a second, unauthoritative statement of role eligibility. No text says it yields to roleEligibility.
- INDEX.md tells readers to fetch from the public main URL when no checkout exists. I used only the local corpus, as instructed.
