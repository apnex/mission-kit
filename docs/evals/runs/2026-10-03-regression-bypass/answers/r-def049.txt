### system-boundary.S6
It is defined in entities/E5-system.md: "A system is a unit of engineering with a boundary: what is inside it, and the contracts through which anything outside reaches it."
The boundary separates it from a neighbour "only where it is declared and honoured".
Systems relate only by containing and composing. A system is "Not a repository, a deployment unit or a process by definition" and is "Not defined by size or by traits."
Traits describe a system and axioms bind it (E5; T0 Purpose). E6 defines a component as "a system seen as a part of a larger system that contains it."

### axiom-direction.Q1
The service is stateful, so A1 binds it: "No functional unit possesses private, opaque, or transient truth" (A1 Mandate). The component's memory-only state is the A1 "Hidden State Problem" fault, and with several agents it also bears on A5.
For your own feature, A0 says "new work never repeats a gap the axiom names". So your feature must put its state in the shared, durable, queryable store and must not add more process-private state. Per the same A0 text, if your feature touches or depends on that component, you must not build on its gap, and you must close as much of it as the touch allows.
If your change does not touch the component, A0 says "Beyond the scope, improvement is considered, never mandated."
If the component is adjacent through a declared interface and passes the A14 tangent test (near payback, yield captured, no significant distraction), you may fix it as a separate change. Otherwise you record it "with its evidence and a revival trigger (M5)", because the director decides what work happens next (R4, AR3).
"Either way the gap is recorded. Passing it silently is what the axiom forbids" (A0). A14 also requires capture at discovery.
NOT SETTLED: whether the component is adjacent to your feature. The prompt does not say.

### axiom-direction.Q2
An axiom is a limit, an "asymptote", that the system is built toward. It is not a pass-or-fail test (A0, "Standing commitment, not situated move"; the CORRECTION banner retracts the invariant reading).
It binds each change in two directions: "No change may move the system away from the limit, and each change moves it toward the limit as far as the change's own scope allows."
A system that is short of the limit is not wrong for that. It is wrong only if it moves away from the limit. So the axiom gives the change a direction, rather than a verdict on the whole system.
Inside the change's scope, the axiom requires you not to repeat any gap you touch and to close what you can. Outside the scope, improvement is opportunistic or recorded, never silently skipped.
A gate checks only the direction rule: that the change "moved the system no further from any limit in force, and repeated no gap it touched" (A0).
A0 names the misreading as "The conformance reading", under which "no change advances the system, and the gaps the axioms name become permanent."
