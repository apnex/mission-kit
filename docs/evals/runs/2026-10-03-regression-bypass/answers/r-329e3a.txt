### component.C2
It is defined in entities/E6-component.md: "A component is a system seen as a part of a larger system that contains it", with "one duty" at that altitude, and it "exposes and consumes interfaces"; its boundary is those interfaces. A system is defined in entities/E5-system.md as "a unit of engineering with a boundary", which separates it from a neighbour only where it is "declared and honoured"; traits describe a system and axioms bind it. They are not different kinds of thing: "Every component is a system; component names its place in a containing system". The difference is the question being asked. "Traits, axioms and what a change answers to are questions about a system. Duty, interfaces, neighbours and blast radius are questions about a component" (E6, "Component against system"). A system that nothing contains "is not a component of anything". A component is not a directory, file, package, repo or SysML part, and it does not have to be registered or shareable (E6, Boundaries).
