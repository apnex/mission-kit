### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. D0 (domains/README.md), "How domains compose": "Domain entries carry lists of the work-types they admit; no rule consults those lists, and where they differ the work-type governs". It repeats this under bimodal freedom ("the lists domains carry are not consulted") and in the fault "The doubly-declared pairing" ("only the work-type's list is consulted"); W0 constraint 3 is the rule that gates on domainEligibility.
(b) The axiom's applies-to governs. A0 (axioms/README.md): "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does", and "An axiom is in force for a system if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies at least one of the axiom's applies-to tags"; T0: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its applies-to field".
No sentence explicitly names the trait-list-versus-applies-to conflict; the answer follows from "the only field that does", not from a stated tie-break.
The five trait lists currently match every applies-to field (checked by reading all fourteen axiom frontmatters). tools/check-traits.mjs checks only the applies-to enum against the trait filenames, not the trait-body lists, so nothing I found enforces that match.

### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 constraint 2 says a collapsed roster should "fail the seed or downgrade to kind:review", and constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation"; for a lone agent, it is not stated whether the kind:review downgrade means review by another identity or a lawful self-review. R0 and constraint 9 point to the former.
- D0 says "A domain cites the axioms it must satisfy", which can be misread as domains bringing axioms into force; D1's A1 citation reads as a rationale for the taxonomy, not as a binding.
- Trait entries carry "Axioms it brings into force" lists that duplicate applies-to; the corpus warns elsewhere that doubly-declared lists drift (the D0 doubly-declared-pairing fault), but no checker I found holds these lists to applies-to.
- W7's title and falsifier are framed for artifacts and channels ("ship to the estate/channel", "live artifact mismatch"), yet it is the only fit for applying a migration on product-data.
- D3's subjectSurface names specific tools ("ois/pi/claude launchers"); D0 already records this as the "domain named after a tool" fault.
- INDEX.md says links resolve against the repository root or a GitHub raw URL; I read only the local corpus copy, as instructed.
