PROBE boundary-bypass.B4

Question:
A check detects that several components bypass T's declared interface to reach T's internals. What does the corpus ask you to do?

Key:
Treat the bypass as a signal, not only as faults in the consumers: evaluate T's interface against T's duty and vision. Either the consumers are wrong and are rerouted onto the interface, or the interface falls short of T's duty - the consumers have an unsignalled change request - and the interface is adjusted. An adjusted interface alters its consumers, so their axioms bind that change.

Rubric:
2 for the interface evaluation against duty and vision with both outcomes. 1 for only 'fix the consumers' grounded in A3, or only 'adjust the interface'. 0 for no action, or for forking or replacing T. Splitting T because the evaluation finds a second duty counts as adjusting the interface.

--- answer a-75efecb6 ---

It says a detected bypass triggers "an evaluation of the interface against the component's duty and its vision, ending in the consumer being moved onto the interface or the interface being adjusted". A3 Success signal 5: "each one detected is evaluated against the component's duty rather than only corrected in the consumer". entities/E6-component.md ("Boundary against directory") says the same thing. If the work falls outside your change's scope, A0 says to record it "with its evidence and a revival trigger" (rules/RU3-anti-amnesia-deferral.md) rather than skip it silently. NOT SETTLED: whether several bypassers change the procedure. GUESS: several consumers bypassing in the same way is stronger evidence that the interface, not the consumers, falls short.

--- answer a-f35da7ba ---
Treat each bypass as a fault in that consumer and as a signal about T's interface (A3 Mechanics, "Bypass as Signal"). Then evaluate T's interface against T's duty and its vision.
The evaluation must end with each consumer moved onto the interface, or with the interface adjusted (A3). A3 Success signal 5 adds that each detected violation "is evaluated against the component's duty rather than only corrected in the consumer".

If you adjust the interface, that change answers to every consumer whose contract it alters, or, for a versioned interface, to each consumer that adopts it (A0, E5).
