### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. domains/README.md (D0) says: "Domain entries carry lists of the work-types they admit; no rule consults those lists, and where they differ the work-type governs". In its bimodal-freedom section it also says "the lists domains carry are not consulted". It names the risk as the fault "The doubly-declared pairing ... only the work-type's list is consulted", and W0 constraint 3 is the rule that gates pairings.
(b) The axiom's applies-to field governs. A0 says "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does", and T0 says "An axiom names the traits it governs in its applies-to field".
Unlike (a), the corpus has no explicit "where they differ" sentence for trait lists. The conclusion follows from the "only field" wording, so it is settled by inference, not stated outright.
No check compares each trait's "Axioms it brings into force" list against applies-to. tools/check-traits.mjs only checks that the applies-to enum matches the set of trait files. I compared T1 to T5 against A1 to A14 by hand, and today they agree.

### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 constraint 2 allows a collapsed roster to "downgrade to kind:review", and constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". Read alone, constraint 2 seems to let a lone agent self-review, so the two read as in tension.
- Domain-side work-type lists are incomplete, as D0 admits. D1 freeForTypes omits W10, W11 and W12, which list delivery-code. D3 lists 7 types, but W4, W5, W6, W7, W10, W11, W12, W14 and W26 also list tooling-harness. D7 matches exactly.
- R3's engagement-mode names W8, W9, W10, W11, W12 and W13, but W16, W20, W22 and W25 also list verifier in roleEligibility.
- W7's hydrate-when ("estate or a release channel") would not route someone applying a migration to records. Only D7's prose links them.
- D1 cites A1 with no qualifier, so a reader could take A1 to bind every codebase. The A0/D0 rule says otherwise, but D1 itself does not say so.
- For trait lists against applies-to, there is no explicit precedence rule and no consistency check, unlike the domain/work-type case.
- D3's subject surface names specific tools (ois/pi/claude), as D0's "domain named after a tool" fault acknowledges.
