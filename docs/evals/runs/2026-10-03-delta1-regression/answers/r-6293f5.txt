### component-a3.C5
The 'utils' module is a named fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns."
Your change must not add to it: "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches" - so the feature goes in its own unit owning one concern ("Law of One - a module does exactly one thing").
If your change touches the utils module, the Mandate asks you to remove the part you touch; the text does not say how much of utils counts as "what it touches" - NOT SETTLED.
The feature should be assembled by composing existing units: "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by modifying them", interacting only "through declared adapters and contracts".
It earns an internal boundary by being one concern, but should not be exposed as a stable surface without "a real consumer outside its origin" ("Earned Exposure"; fault "Speculative Surface").
You are not asked to refactor all of utils if your change does not touch it: the change "removes what it touches", not everything. GUESS: an untouched remainder of utils stays as known debt.
Exactly how an axiom binds a change is deferred to A0 ("How an axiom binds a change is stated in A0"), which I was not permitted to read - NOT SETTLED beyond the Mandate's sentence.
