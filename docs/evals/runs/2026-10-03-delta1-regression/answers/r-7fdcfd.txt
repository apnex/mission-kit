### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 constraint 2 says that when the roster collapses attester and executor, the gate may "downgrade to kind:review". Constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". If the downgraded kind:review is done by the same agent, the two contradict each other. The text does not say who performs the downgraded review.
- Nothing I saw mechanically keeps trait "Axioms it brings into force" lists in sync with applies-to. tools/check-traits.mjs (first 40 lines read) only checks the applies-to enum against the declared trait names. This drift could happen silently. I inferred this from a partial read.
- D1's freeForTypes leaves out W10, W11 and W12, even though each of their domainEligibility lists includes delivery-code. D0 says this is expected ("every disagreement an omission on the domain side"), but it is a trap for anyone who reads D1 alone.
- W25 names "a migration" as a typical risk window, but its domain is pinned to coordination-substrate. A backstop over a product-data change therefore lands on a different domain than the work it brackets. This is consistent with the "type names its own surface" rule, but it is easy to misread.
- D1 cites A1 in its axiom alignment, even though A1 binds only stateful systems. A reader could take that citation to mean A1 binds all codebase work. A0 and D0 say otherwise, but D1 itself does not.
- T4 autonomous cannot be judged from P1's or P2's descriptions. In both cases it made no difference to the result.
