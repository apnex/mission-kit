Method: axioms/README.md (A0) says an axiom binds a system "if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies at least one of the axiom's applies-to tags", tags are alternatives, and traits/README.md says obligations are "the union of every axiom any of its traits brings into force, plus every axiom on the floor". Result: A1, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13, A14 - thirteen, everything except A2. A2 (applies-to: [declarative]) is NOT SETTLED: the question does not say whether the service reconciles toward a declared spec (T2 test); if it does, A2 binds too and all fourteen apply. Whether it is T4 autonomous is also not stated, but it changes nothing, since A7/A10/A13 already bind via multi-agent.

### composition.P2
Traits: not stateful, not declarative ("ordinary script"; T2 lists "a script run once" as not declarative), not multi-agent (one actor, T3), no model (T5), and run by a person, so not autonomous (T4 test). So only the floor binds: A3, A4, A8, A9, A14 (A0 matrix row any-system; E5: "A one-shot script with no traits is a system, bound by the axioms every system carries"). A1 does not constrain this work. A0: "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does... a domain entry cites the axioms it must satisfy"; D0: "applies-to decides whether an axiom binds, and the domain decides what it demands on that surface". A1's applies-to is [stateful] and the formatter is not stateful, so D1's citation of A1 (domains/D1-delivery-code.md, "Axiom alignment") only says what A1 would require on that surface for a system it binds. It does not put A1 in force here.

### composition.P5
No. W8 is verifier-only and its falsifier is "self-attestation". R0 says "The same identity may not be both the executor and the independent verifier of one piece of work", and R3 says it "may never attest own executed work". ### composition.P7
(a) The work-type governs. domains/README.md, "How domains compose": "Domain entries carry lists of the work-types they admit; no rule consults those lists, and where they differ the work-type governs". It repeats this under "Bimodal freedom" ("the lists domains carry are not consulted") and in the fault "The doubly-declared pairing" ("only the work-type's list is consulted"); W0 constraint 3 enforces the work-type's domainEligibility. (b) The axiom's applies-to governs. A0 (axioms/README.md, "What brings an axiom into force") says "applies-to answers whether an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does", and T0 says "An axiom names the traits it governs in its applies-to field". Unlike (a), no sentence explicitly addresses a disagreement between a trait's list and applies-to. The ruling follows from "the only field that does", so I count it as settled by implication, not by a stated tie-break. I measured the five trait lists against the axioms' applies-to frontmatter and they currently agree.

### OBSERVATIONS
- The trait entries' "Axioms it brings into force" lists duplicate the axioms' applies-to: the same doubly-declared shape D0 names as a fault for domains. tools/check-traits.mjs checks only the tag vocabulary against the schema enum, not these per-trait lists. I found no checker that keeps them in sync; I only searched tools/ filenames and grepped for "brings into force", so this is about my search, not proof.
- Domain freeForTypes lists leave out pairings the work-types allow. D1 lacks run-a-live-probe-or-smoke (W11), adversarial-design-review-upstream (W10) and meta-validate-dogfood (W12), all of which admit delivery-code. D3 lacks merge-and-land (W6), publish-deploy (W7), author-guard tests (W5) and reset-or-converge-the-fleet (W26), all of which admit tooling-harness. This fits D0's statement that the domain-side lists disagree by omission and are not consulted.
- W0 constraint 2 says that if the roster collapses attester and executor, "fail the seed or downgrade to kind:review", while constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". Read alone, constraint 2 could suggest a lone agent may downgrade to a self-held kind:review. Only constraint 9 rules that out.
- No entry explicitly maps "apply an already-merged migration" to a work-type. W7's hydrate-when and definition talk about shipping to "the estate/channel", which fits a database migration awkwardly.
- D3's subject surface names tools (ois/pi/claude launchers), which D0 itself records as the "domain named after a tool" fault.
