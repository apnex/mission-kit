### component-a3.C5
Do not put the feature into `utils`. The file names this exact shape as a fault: "God-Object Accretion - "utils", "helpers", "managers" accumulate unrelated concerns." The Mandate says "a change short of it adds none of them, and removes what it touches", so your change must not add to the dual-purpose module. Give the feature its own unit under the "Law of One" ("a module does exactly one thing"), and build it by composition: "a new capability is assembled by composing existing units, never by modifying them." If you consume anything from `utils`, go only "through declared adapters and contracts". Do not promote the new unit to a stable public surface unless "a real consumer outside its origin needs it". NOT SETTLED: how far "removes what it touches" goes. GUESS: if your change edits or depends on part of `utils`, you should extract that part into its own single-concern unit, but you do not have to break up the whole module.

### OBSERVATIONS
- "removes what it touches" is unclear: it does not say whether "touches" means editing the module, depending on it, or only the lines changed. So the cleanup a small change owes is not defined.
- "Composable by Default" ("never by modifying them") seems to conflict with "removes what it touches". Removing accretion from `utils` means modifying an existing unit. The file does not say which rule wins.
- The Success signals defer to `A0` ("How an axiom binds a change is stated in A0"), and that file was out of scope. So the exact obligation on a partial change may be stated somewhere I could not read.
