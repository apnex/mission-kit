### composition.P1
A1, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13, A14 bind it - every axiom except A2. Method: `axioms/README.md` (A0) says an axiom is in force "if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies at least one of the axiom's `applies-to` tags", and `traits/README.md` (T0) says "a system's obligations are the union of every axiom any of its traits brings into force, plus every axiom on the floor". I applied the trait tests: T1 stateful (persistent state) brings A1; T3 multi-agent (several cooperating agents, work crossing between them) brings A5, A6, A7, A10, A13; T5 llm-in-the-loop (model deciding on the critical path) brings A5, A11, A12. The `any-system` floor adds A3, A4, A8, A9, A14 (A0 applicability matrix), and I checked each axiom's own `applies-to` field against this. T4 autonomous would add nothing new (A7, A10, A13 already bind through T3). Whether it is T2 declarative is NOT SETTLED by the description, so A2 is in force only if it reconciles toward a declared spec (T2 test). Per A0 these bind as asymptotes: no change may move away from a limit, and each change moves toward it within its own scope.

### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. D0 *How domains compose*: "Domain entries carry lists of the work-types they admit; no rule consults those lists, and where they differ the work-type governs"; D0 *Bimodal freedom* adds "the lists domains carry are not consulted", and the D0 fault "The doubly-declared pairing" says "only the work-type's list is consulted". W0 constraint 3 is the rule that does the gating, and it reads `domainEligibility`. (b) The axiom's `applies-to` governs. A0 *What brings an axiom into force*: "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does", and T0 *Purpose*: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its `applies-to` field." No passage addresses a disagreement between the two lists directly; the "only field" sentence settles it by implication. I checked T1 to T5 and today their lists match the axioms' `applies-to` values.

### OBSERVATIONS
- W0 constraint 2 says that if the roster collapses attester and executor, "fail the seed or downgrade to `kind:review`"; constraint 9 and R0 say "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". For a lone agent the `kind:review` downgrade is ambiguous: whose review, and does it count?
- The trait body contract (`schemas/entry-body/v1alpha1/entry-body.json`, trait rationale) says "A trait decides whether axioms bind", while A0 says `applies-to` "is the only field that does". Trait lists ("Axioms it brings into force") are a second copy of `applies-to`, and nothing enforces them: `tools/check-traits.mjs` only checks the enum against `traits/` filenames. This is the same doubly-declared shape D0 names as a fault for domain lists.
- W7's evidence contract ("release artifact + deploy log", "version/digest/SHA + rollback anchor") does not match D7's evidence ("the records before and after, migration and repair logs, row counts and integrity checks"), so it is unclear what closes a W7 x D7 migration node. W7's body never mentions data.
- R3's engagement-mode lists meta-validate-dogfood and code-owner-approve but not W20/W25/W16/W22, though those work-types list verifier in `roleEligibility`. No rule is stated for role-side lists the way D0 states one for domain-side lists.
- D0 says "A domain cites the axioms it must satisfy", which a reader can take to mean the axiom binds any work on that domain (see P2). A0 and D0's orthogonality text say otherwise, but the phrase invites the misreading.
- INDEX.md tells the reader to resolve links against a remote GitHub URL when no checkout is present; in this sandboxed corpus I used the local files only.
