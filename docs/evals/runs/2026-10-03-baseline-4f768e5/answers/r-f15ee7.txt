### system-boundary.S6
It is defined in `entities/E5-system.md`: "A system is a unit of engineering with a boundary: what is inside it, and the contracts through which anything outside reaches it."
"The boundary separates it from its neighbours only where it is declared; where one unit depends on another across a boundary nobody declared, the two are one system for deciding what a change answers to."
Systems relate in exactly two ways, contain and compose; a system is not a repository, deployment unit or process by definition, and not defined by size or traits.
"Traits describe a system, and axioms bind it"; a component is "a system seen as a part of a larger system that contains it" (`entities/E6-component.md`).

### axiom-direction.Q1
The service is stateful and multi-agent, so A1 and A5 bind it; that component is short of A1 ("No functional unit possesses private, opaque, or transient truth"; fault "Hidden State Problem - state lives inside a process; other actors reason about a different reality").
Whether you must fix it depends on scope (`axioms/README.md`): you must improve what your change touches - "what it modifies, what it newly depends on, and any consumer whose contract it alters" - and "Beyond the scope, improvement is considered, never mandated."
If your feature newly depends on the component, it is touched but not altered, "so its gaps must not be built on" - do not read or rely on its hidden in-memory state.
If it is adjacent (shares a declared interface, `entities/E6-component.md`), you may close the gap opportunistically under A14 tangent discipline, as a separate change; otherwise record it "with its evidence and a revival trigger (M5)", since what work happens next is the director's call (R4, AR3).
Either way "the gap is recorded"; "Passing it silently is what the axiom forbids."
For your own feature: "new work never repeats a gap the axiom names", so keep its state in the shared, durable, queryable backplane (A1 mechanics: "State is a physical object in the backplane, not a variable living inside a process"), visible to the other agents (A5).

### axiom-direction.Q2
An axiom is a direction, not a pass/fail test: it is "a load-bearing asymptote - a limit a system is built toward" (`axioms/README.md`).
When you change a bound system it binds that change two ways at once: "No change may move the system away from the limit, and each change moves it toward the limit as far as the change's own scope allows."
A system short of the limit is not wrong for that; "it is wrong for moving away from it."
The CORRECTION banner in `axioms/README.md` retracts the earlier "hold or fail" wording, and the fault "The conformance reading" explains why: treated as pass/fail, "no change advances the system, and the gaps the axioms name become permanent."
Gates fit this: they check that the change "moved the system no further from any limit in force, and repeated no gap it touched." Axioms in force also cannot be traded off against each other ("no axiom may be traded against another").
