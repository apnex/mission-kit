### composition.P1
Three traits apply on their tests: `T1` stateful (writes state it reads back), `T3` multi-agent (several cooperating agents' work crosses between them), `T5` llm-in-the-loop (a model decides on the critical path).
Binding rule: an axiom "is in force for a system if, and only if, that system's architecture satisfies at least one of the axiom's `applies-to` tags", and tags "are alternatives, not a conjunction" (`axioms/README.md`, "What brings an axiom into force"); `traits/README.md` adds "a system's obligations are the union of every axiom any of its traits brings into force, plus every axiom on the floor".
From the `applies-to` fields: stateful -> A1; multi-agent -> A5, A6, A7, A10, A13; llm-in-the-loop -> A5, A11, A12; floor `any-system` -> A3, A4, A8, A9, A14.
Union: A1, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A9, A10, A11, A12, A13, A14 - all except A2.
I checked this against the applicability matrix in `axioms/README.md` and the "Axioms it brings into force" sections of T1, T3 and T5, which agree.
A2 binds only `declarative` systems (`T2`), and the question does not say whether the service reconciles toward a declared spec, so A2 is NOT SETTLED.
GUESS: "long-running" probably also makes it `T4` autonomous, but that adds nothing, because A7, A10 and A13 already bind through multi-agent.

### composition.P2

So only the floor binds: A3, A4, A8, A9, A14 (`axioms/README.md` matrix, "a plain library takes `any-system`"; `E5`: "A one-shot script with no traits is a system, bound by the axioms every system carries").

### composition.P3
Work-type: `W7` publish-deploy-or-canonicalize, which is "the artifact/live-plane act", `domainEligibility: [distribution, tooling-harness, product-data]`, and `dependsOn the landed change (W6 merge-and-land)`.
Domain: `D7` product-data, which says "writing it is `delivery-code`, and applying it to the records is `product-data`" and pairs that split with merge-and-land versus publish-deploy; `D0`'s tie-break puts "applying a migration" on "the state the action changes".
Role: `R2` engineer, because W7's `roleEligibility` is `[engineer]` and its `evidenceAuthority` is `executor-evidence`.
Evidence: deploy log plus a rollback anchor, and the W7 falsifier is "live artifact mismatch or rollback path absent".
D7's A9 citation adds that the migration must be proven in a sandbox, against interruption and concurrent writes, with a restore point, before it touches real records.
GUESS: an independent gate (`W8` on product-data, verifier `R3`) or a `W25` backstop could bracket it, but the corpus does not require either for this task.

### composition.P5
No.
`W8` is "gating a build or change that you did not author", and its falsifier is "self-attestation".

### composition.P6
`D3` matches directly: "You are changing the harness that launches or hosts the runtime".
`D0` matches because you are placing work on the domain axis; its tie-break keeps a harness change on tooling-harness even when the change serves the service.
`W0` matches because you are classifying the unit of work. Then read the matching work-type: `W1` if you are "scoping the implementation of a bounded increment", or `W2` if it is a filed defect, and `W4` when you self-check the result.
`A0` and `T0` match because you must decide "which axioms bind the system in front of you". `T1` matches because the service "holds state that something else depends on being true". `T3`, `T4` and `T5` match if the harness's agents hand work across, run unattended, or put a model on the path.
`E5` matches because you are "deciding which systems' axioms a change answers to"; it says "A launcher that hands sessions to a service through a declared interface composes with it", so the service's A1 binds only if the change alters that contract or the boundary is undeclared. `E6` matches for "what a change can reach".
`AR2` matches because you are "about to change a system and need the change declared and its landing provable", and `R0` matches when deciding who may attest the result.
GUESS: `A1` and `A7` would also match if the change touches how state is seen or how failed launches behave, but whether they apply is NOT SETTLED from the question alone.

### composition.P7
(a) The work-type's list governs. `D0`, "How domains compose": "Domain entries carry lists of the work-types they admit; no rule consults those lists, and where they differ the work-type governs". It repeats this under "Bimodal freedom" ("the lists domains carry are not consulted") and under the fault "The doubly-declared pairing" ("only the work-type's list is consulted"). `W0` constraint 3 enforces it.
(b) The axiom's `applies-to` would govern. `axioms/README.md`: "`applies-to` answers *whether* an axiom binds, and it is the only field that does". `traits/README.md`: "An axiom names the traits it governs in its `applies-to` field".
No sentence says explicitly "if a trait's list disagrees, applies-to wins", so that precedence is inferred from the "only field" rule, and an explicit disagreement rule is NOT SETTLED.
Measured today, the five trait lists match the `applies-to` fields exactly.

### OBSERVATIONS
- `W0` constraint 2 says that when the roster collapses attester and executor, the seed should "fail the seed or downgrade to `kind:review`". Constraint 9 and `R0` say "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". For a lone agent these two rules point different ways, unless a downgraded `kind:review` is meant to be held by someone else; the text does not say which.
- Each trait's "Axioms it brings into force" list is a second, hand-maintained copy of the axioms' `applies-to` fields. `tools/check-traits.mjs` checks only the tag vocabulary against the enum, not these per-trait lists, and no rule states which copy wins. They agree today.
- Trait lists omit the floor axioms (A3, A4, A8, A9, A14). A reader who adds up trait sections alone gets an incomplete set; only `T0` mentions "plus every axiom on the floor".
- `R3`'s engagement-mode list does not mention W20, W22 or W25, even though all three list `verifier` in `roleEligibility`.
- `W7`'s evidence contract ("release artifact", "version/digest/SHA") is written for releases and fits a data migration awkwardly, although `D7` routes migrations to it. Its evidence does not name D7's own evidence (records before and after, row counts, integrity checks).
- A9 Chaos-Validated Deployment binds every system, including a single-user CLI script, which reads oddly. `axioms/README.md` itself flags A9 as the least exercised axiom.
- "Run by one person" leaves `T4` slightly open, because the test asks whether a human sees a failure before the system acts on it. I read an interactive CLI as not autonomous.
