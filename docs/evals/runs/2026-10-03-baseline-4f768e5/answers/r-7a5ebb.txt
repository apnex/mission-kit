### OBSERVATIONS
- `work-types/README.md` constraint 2 says that if the roster collapses attester and executor, you "fail the seed or downgrade to `kind:review`". Constraint 9 says "Same-agent review is never a valid degradation". Constraint 2 does not say who performs the downgraded review, so a reader of constraint 2 alone could self-review.
- `roles/R3-verifier.md`'s engagement-mode lists six work-types. The `roleEligibility` unions also make the verifier eligible for W16, W20, W22 and W25, which R3 does not list.
- D1's A1 alignment rationale (PR/commit/CI node truth) is about the work-tracking system rather than the product codebase. Domains carry no `applies-to`, so a domain's axiom citations can easily be misread as binding (P2).
- Domain `freeForTypes` lists omit many eligible pairings. D1 omits W10, W11 and W12. D3 omits W5, W6, W7, W10, W12, W14 and W26. This is consistent with D0's recorded fault, but the stale lists are still in the entries.
- W7's text speaks only of release artifacts and channels. Applying a migration to records is tied to W7 only through `domainEligibility` and D7's analogy.
- No checker or rule covers disagreement between trait-body axiom lists and `applies-to`. They currently agree, but that agreement is held by nothing.
- `INDEX.md` tells readers to resolve links against GitHub `main` when there is no local checkout. In this corpus copy I resolved links locally only.
