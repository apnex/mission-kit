### OBSERVATIONS
- `artifacts/README.md` (AR0) opens by calling the types "each with a schema". `schemas/README.md` says lifecycle documents "have no contract at all", only prose templates. AR1 and AR6 ship no template. So "schema" in AR0 is either loose wording or wrong.
- `work-types/README.md` hard-codes counts ("DOMAINS (N=7)", "K~=26"). These go stale when the D0 or W0 territories grow, which both charters say they will.
- W0's corpus-extension gap speaks of "adding or retiring a layer" and does not cover revising or retiring an individual entry. W3's definition says "from the codebase", but its `domainEligibility` includes knowledge-methodology.
- The root `README.md` says `INDEX.md` is "the flat ledger", but INDEX.md is grouped by layer.
- `methods/README.md` is missing a blank line before the `---` that follows "How the procedures compose" (a minor formatting slip).
- The root `README.md` links to `docs/README.md`, but no `docs/` directory exists in this corpus copy.
