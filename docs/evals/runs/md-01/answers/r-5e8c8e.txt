### charter-claims.V1
No, the charter should not narrow its claim. entities/E4-charter.md says "A charter holds the set's vision, and manages the set against it", and the population "is never cut to fit what the set holds today". It adds: "A claim no member yet fills is a gap ... and naming it is how gaps are found". ### charter-claims.V6
No, not in itself. domains/README.md (D0) says "The set grows when the territory finds a gap. This set is not complete and is not expected to be". The charter sets conditions. The new domain must close a surface "that no domain resolves" and must come "with its own evidence contract, not by stretching a neighbour". It also needs a distinct evidence contract, or it falls into the "indistinct pair" fault. If it fits none of the existing parts, "the part is added with the domain". product-data (D7) is the example of a domain that "entered this way". An eighth domain that duplicated an existing surface's evidence, or that encoded a work mode ("The mode-domain" fault), would contradict the charter.

### charter-claims.V7

### OBSERVATIONS
- artifacts/README.md (AR0) opens with "The document types an engineering lifecycle produces, each with a schema". schemas/README.md says lifecycle documents have no contract, only prose templates, and AR1 and AR6 ship no template at all. Those two statements conflict.
- README.md links `docs/README.md` in its layer table, but this corpus copy has no `docs/` directory.
- README.md "Adding an entry" step 5 says to add the entry to INDEX.md and to the category README. Both indexes are marked "generated ... do not edit by hand", and README's own fault "The typed index" warns against hand-maintained ledgers.
- W0's "W3 to retire" guidance is about layers. W3 defines a hard cut, which does not match how the corpus retires entries (by supersession, keeping the ID).
- E3 and E4 say "thirteen charters". The corpus now has sixteen ID-bearing layer charters (A, R, D, W, M, RU, PC, ST, S, P, K, E, C, AR, T, SC). That figure may be historical, but nothing labels it that way.
- W0 hard-codes "DOMAINS (N=7)" and "K~=26". These counts go stale as soon as a domain or work-type is added.
- In methods/README.md, a "---" line comes directly after a text line with no blank line between them (end of "How the procedures compose"). In Markdown this may render as a setext heading instead of a rule.
