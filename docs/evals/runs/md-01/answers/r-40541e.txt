### charter-claims.V1
No. `entities/E4-charter.md` says the vision "is never cut to fit what the set holds today", and "A claim no member yet fills is a gap - the distance between the population and its charter - and naming it is how gaps are found."
On territory it adds: "How far the territory reaches is set by what the set is for, not by what it holds: a cell no member fills is kept and named as a gap, and removing it to match the population erases the gap it exposes."
Stating scope is not all a charter does. E4 says "A charter holds the set's vision, and manages the set against it".
The management part is: the territory shows where the population stands, the boundaries decide what enters and where the rest goes, the faults say how the set goes wrong, and changes are made under "Changing a charter".
E4's required concerns are Purpose, Territory, Boundaries, Composition, Faults and Index. It also lists conditional concerns (Kinds, Member shape, Placement, Enforcement boundary, Sub-sets, Axiom alignment, Probes) and free sections.
A territory must also "show a gap or argue that there is none" (E4, Discriminators).

### OBSERVATIONS
- Root `README.md` lists a `docs/` layer ("This corpus's own artifact instances"), but this corpus copy has no `docs/` directory.
- Root `README.md` calls `INDEX.md` "the flat ledger", but `INDEX.md` is grouped by layer.
- Root `README.md` "Adding an entry" step 5 says to add the entry to `INDEX.md` and the category README. Those index regions are marked "GENERATED ... do not edit by hand".
- `INDEX.md` tells the reader to fetch from GitHub `main` when there is no local checkout. That is outside the corpus this task allows.
- `artifacts/README.md` says the document types each come "with a schema". `schemas/README.md` says lifecycle documents have no contract and only prose templates. These conflict unless "schema" means template.
- `work-types/README.md` hard-codes counts ("DOMAINS (N=7)", "K~=26"). They will drift when D0 grows the set as it expects to.
- `work-types/W3-retire-or-hard-cut.md` is defined in terms of codebase surfaces, yet it is the work-type W0 names for retiring corpus layers.
- `rules/README.md` lists "credentials are never committed" as a rule-less candidate under *Treating the record*, a moment that already has rule members (RU2, RU4). So it is a missing member, not an empty moment.
