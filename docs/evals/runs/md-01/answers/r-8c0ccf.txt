### charter-claims.V1
No. The charter should not narrow its claim. `entities/E4-charter.md` (Definition) says the vision "is what the set is for and what it claims to cover; the population is measured against it ... and it is never cut to fit what the set holds today", and "A claim no member yet fills is a gap ... and naming it is how gaps are found." Its Discriminators section adds that a territory's "extent is set by purpose", and that "a cell no member fills is kept and named as a gap, and removing it to match the population erases the gap it exposes."
Stating the scope is not all a charter does. E4 says "A charter holds the set's vision, and manages the set against it". The rest is management: the territory shows where the population stands against the claim, boundaries decide what enters and where the rest goes, faults say how the set goes wrong, and changes follow "Changing a charter". The required concerns are Purpose, Territory, Boundaries, Composition, Faults and Index, and there are conditional concerns too, such as Kinds, Member shape and Probes.

### OBSERVATIONS
- `work-types/README.md` "Gaps recorded" contradicts itself in two sentences next to each other. One says "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." The next says "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." It is unclear whether the governance/coordination-machinery gap is open or settled.
- `artifacts/README.md` opens with "The document types ... each with a schema". `schemas/README.md` says lifecycle documents have "no contract at all", only prose templates. The word "schema" means different things in the two files.
- `work-types/README.md` fixes "DOMAINS (N=7)" and "ROLES (M=4)". `domains/README.md` and `roles/README.md` say their counts follow the territory and may grow.
- `entities/E4-charter.md` and `E3-set.md` refer to "thirteen charters". INDEX now lists sixteen `0`-slot charters (A0, R0, D0, W0, M0, RU0, PC0, ST0, S0, P0, K0, E0, C0, AR0, T0, SC0). The figure is probably historical, but nothing marks it as such.
- In `methods/README.md`, the "How the procedures compose" section ends with a `---` that has no blank line before it (line 110-111). In markdown this can render the preceding line as a setext heading.
