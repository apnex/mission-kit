### OBSERVATIONS
- `work-types/README.md` contradicts itself on governance/coordination machinery. One sentence says "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this.", and the next says "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." Both cannot stand.
- `artifacts/README.md` opens by saying the document types come "each with a schema", but `schemas/README.md` says lifecycle documents have no contract and the types carry only prose templates.
- `practices/README.md` places `PC1` (author from exemplar, whose trigger is adding an entry to a curated collection) at "Entering". That fits M0's "how to begin in an unfamiliar collection" only loosely.
- `INDEX.md` line 24 points readers to fetch from a remote GitHub URL when no checkout is present. This corpus copy is meant to be read in isolation.
- `entities/E3-set.md` and `entities/E4-charter.md` say "thirteen charters", but INDEX lists more `0`-slot charters than that (A0, R0, D0, W0, M0, RU0, PC0, ST0, S0, P0, K0, E0, C0, AR0, T0, SC0). The count may be historical.
