### OBSERVATIONS
- work-types/README.md "Gaps recorded" contradicts itself in two consecutive sentences: "The domains set has not said which, and its answer settles this." and then "The domains charter's tie-break, applied to one such change, settles which." The second sentence also leaves the actual answer unstated.
- work-types/README.md Territory says the Build group "changes a system - the product, the harness, or the organisation's own machinery and knowledge". But no Build work-type (W1, W2, W3, W5) is eligible on knowledge-methodology, and the same charter records that as a gap.
- artifacts/README.md opens with "The document types an engineering lifecycle produces, each with a schema". schemas/README.md says lifecycle documents have no contract and the artifact types "carry prose templates". This conflicts unless "schema" in AR0 means a prose template.
- work-types/README.md fixes counts ("DOMAINS (N=7)", "K~=26"), while domains/README.md and W0 itself say their sets are not complete and are expected to grow. These counts will drift.
- E3 and E4 both say "Thirteen charters in this corpus". INDEX.md now lists 16 layer charters (A0, R0, D0, W0, M0, RU0, PC0, ST0, S0, P0, K0, E0, C0, AR0, T0, SC0). This is inferred from the ledger; the "thirteen" may be historical, but it reads as current.
- methods/README.md: in "How the procedures compose", the line ending "A procedure that cuts or defers anything is held by them." is directly followed by "---" with no blank line. In CommonMark this renders that sentence as a setext H2 heading instead of a horizontal rule.
- ST0's verification gap and R0's "assurance" gap for an organisation of one overlap, and neither cross-references the other.
