# Practices - the how-you-should layer

## Purpose

A **practice** governs how work is done, produces nothing of its own, and **leaves no trace**: nothing in the record afterwards shows whether it was followed.

The set exists because some of the guidance that most changes work cannot be checked, and filing it beside rules would make every rule look as unverifiable as it is.\
A practice earns its place only by evidence that following it produces measurably different work.

---

## Placement

Placement is stated once, in [`M0`](../methods/README.md): axiom, procedure, style, rule, practice, asked in that order.\
A practice is what is left: it produces nothing of its own, does not govern an artifact's form, and leaves no trace a check could test.

**Gaps tested.**\
*Verifying*, *deciding what to keep* and *handing over* hold no practice today.\
A practice is not minted to fill a moment: it is admitted when a difference in the work has been observed.

---

## Member shape

A practice states the situation, the guidance, and the evidence that following it changes the work.\
It declares no `trace`; if it acquires one, it is a rule and moves.\
Placement and admission are separate questions: placement says guidance *is* a practice; admission says whether it has earned an entry, which takes observed evidence.

---

## Boundaries and composition

- **Against rules:** a rule leaves a trace a check could test; a practice does not.
- **Against methods:** a method produces a result of its own.
- **Against style:** style governs an artifact's form.
- **With methods and rules:** a practice often sits inside a method's step, as judgement the step cannot specify.

---

## Faults

- **The practice with a trace.** Guidance that could be checked, filed here, where nothing will check it; it is a rule.
- **The unevidenced practice.** A habit admitted for seeming wise, with no observed difference in the work; it is a proposal.
- **The practice nobody follows.** One whose situation recurs and whose guidance is never applied; it should be revised or retired.
